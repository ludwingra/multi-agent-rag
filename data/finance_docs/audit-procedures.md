## Overview

TechNova Solutions undergoes an **annual external financial audit** conducted by Deloitte, as well as quarterly internal reviews managed by the Finance team. This document outlines the procedures, timelines, and responsibilities for both audit types to ensure TechNova maintains clean financial records and full regulatory compliance as a Delaware C-Corp.

---

## Annual External Audit (Deloitte)

TechNova Solutions engages Deloitte as its external audit firm. The audit covers the full calendar fiscal year (January 1 – December 31) and results in audited financial statements presented to the Board of Directors and investors.

### Audit Timeline

| Phase | Dates | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Planning & Risk Assessment | May 15 – May 31 | Deloitte kick-off meeting, audit scope confirmed |
| Interim Fieldwork | June 1 – June 30 | Controls testing, interim transactions sampling |
| Year-End Fieldwork | January 15 – February 28 | Full-year substantive procedures |
| Draft Financials | March 1 – March 15 | Management review of draft audit report |
| Final Audit Report | March 31 | Issued to Board and key investors |

### Areas of Audit Focus

- Revenue recognition (Stripe / ASC 606 compliance) — see [Revenue Recognition](revenue-recognition.md)
- Accounts receivable and deferred revenue
- Accounts payable and accrued liabilities
- Equity and stock-based compensation (Carta cap table)
- Tax provisions (federal, state, UK subsidiary)
- Internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR)

### Employee Responsibilities During External Audit

- Respond to Deloitte information requests within **3 business days**
- Provide supporting documentation for sampled transactions (invoices, contracts, receipts)
- Do not alter or delete records — any concerns about record accuracy must be escalated to the CFO immediately

## Quarterly Internal Reviews

Finance conducts internal reviews at the end of each quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30, December 31). These are lighter-touch than the external audit but cover:

| Area | Reviewer | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Budget vs. actual variance | Finance Analyst | Quarterly |
| Expense report compliance | Finance Controller | Quarterly |
| Corporate card reconciliation | Finance Analyst | Monthly + Quarterly |
| Vendor payment compliance | AP Manager | Quarterly |
| Subscription registry audit | IT + Finance | Quarterly |

Internal review results are shared with VPs within **10 business days** of quarter-end.

## Internal Controls Framework

TechNova Solutions maintains the following key internal controls:

- **Segregation of duties:** No single employee can both approve a PO and approve the corresponding payment in Bill.com.
- **Dual approval for wire transfers** over $50,000 (requires two Finance team signatories).
- **NetSuite access controls:** Role-based access reviewed quarterly; terminated employee access revoked within 24 hours.
- **Expense sampling:** Finance randomly audits 10% of submitted expense reports each quarter.
- **Detect-secrets:** Finance team uses detect-secrets scans on any code or configuration files containing financial credentials.

## Document Retention for Audit

Per IRS requirements and audit best practices, TechNova retains:

| Document Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Tax returns and supporting docs | 7 years |
| Payroll records | 7 years |
| Vendor invoices and POs | 7 years |
| Contracts and agreements | 10 years (or 3 years post-expiry, whichever is longer) |
| Board minutes and resolutions | Permanent |
| Bank statements | 7 years |

All records are stored in NetSuite (financial) and Google Drive (contracts, correspondence). Physical documents are digitized and destroyed per TechNova's data retention policy.

## Whistleblower and Fraud Reporting

TechNova Solutions maintains a confidential fraud hotline managed by a third-party provider. Employees, contractors, and vendors may report suspected financial misconduct anonymously at:

- **Hotline:** 1-800-TNS-SAFE
- **Email:** ethics@technova.com (monitored by Audit Committee)

All reports are investigated by the Audit Committee of the Board. Retaliation against whistleblowers is prohibited and grounds for termination.
