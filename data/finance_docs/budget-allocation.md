## Overview

TechNova Solutions operates on a **calendar fiscal year** (January 1 – December 31). Annual budgets are planned during Q4 of the preceding year and formally approved by the Board of Directors no later than **December 15**. This document describes how budgets are allocated, tracked, and managed across departments.

---

## Annual Budget Planning Cycle

| Activity | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Finance issues budget templates to department heads | October 1 |
| Department heads submit initial budget proposals | October 31 |
| CFO and VP review sessions | November 1–15 |
| Executive leadership final review | November 20 |
| Board approval | December 15 |
| Budget locked and loaded into NetSuite | January 2 |

## Budget Categories

Within each cost center, budgets are divided into:

- **Personnel Costs** — Salaries, benefits, payroll taxes (via Gusto). Approximately 65% of total operating expenses company-wide.
- **Cloud Infrastructure** — AWS, GCP, Cloudflare, and other hosting costs. Target: ~12% of revenue ($10.2M in FY2025 at $85M ARR).
- **Software & SaaS** — Approved subscriptions tracked in the [Subscription Management](subscription-management.md) registry.
- **T&E** — Travel and entertainment; governed by [Travel Policy](travel-policy.md).
- **Professional Services** — Legal, accounting (Deloitte), recruiting agencies, and consulting.
- **CapEx** — Hardware and equipment; subject to [Equipment Depreciation](equipment-depreciation.md) rules.

## Mid-Year Budget Adjustments

Budget reallocation requests (transfers between line items or cost centers) follow the same approval chain as purchases:

| Transfer Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $10,000 | Department Director |
| $10,000 – $49,999 | CFO |
| $50,000+ | CFO + Board notification |

Requests must be submitted via the Finance portal with a written justification and the business impact of not reallocating.

---

## Variance Reporting

Finance produces a **monthly budget-vs-actual report** distributed to all VPs and Directors by the **10th business day** of the following month. Reports are generated from NetSuite and cover:

- Actual spend vs. budget by cost center
- Forecast vs. plan (rolling 3-month)
- Headcount actuals vs. plan
- Cloud infrastructure spend vs. 12% revenue target

Departments showing variance of **>10% unfavorable** for two consecutive months are required to submit a corrective action plan to the CFO within 15 days.

---

## Capital Expenditure (CapEx) Budget

TechNova Solutions maintains a separate CapEx budget approved by the Board. FY2025 CapEx budget is **$1.2M**, covering office equipment, lab hardware for engineering, and leasehold improvements. All CapEx purchases require a [Purchase Requisition](purchase-requisition.md) and are tracked per [Equipment Depreciation](equipment-depreciation.md) policy.

---

## Budget Freeze Conditions

The CFO may declare a budget freeze at any time if actual cash runway falls below **12 months**. During a freeze, all non-essential purchases over $1,000 require CFO approval regardless of cost center.
