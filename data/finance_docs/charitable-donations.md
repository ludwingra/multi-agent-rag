# Charitable Donations Policy

**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Version:** 1.3

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions is committed to making a positive impact in the communities where we operate — San Francisco, Austin, London, and Toronto. This policy governs corporate charitable giving, employee gift matching, and in-kind donations to ensure all contributions align with TechNova's values, comply with IRS rules, and are properly recorded in NetSuite for financial reporting and tax purposes.

---

## Corporate Charitable Giving Budget

TechNova Solutions maintains a dedicated charitable giving budget approved annually as part of the [Budget Allocation](budget-allocation.md) process:

| Category | FY2025 Budget | Cost Center |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate cash donations | $150,000 | GNA |
| Employee gift matching program | $100,000 | GNA |
| In-kind donations (software, services) | $50,000 | GNA |
| **Total** | **$300,000** | GNA |

---

## Eligible Organizations

TechNova Solutions will only make charitable contributions to:

- US organizations with confirmed **501(c)(3)** status (IRS Exempt Organizations Search)
- UK registered charities (Charity Commission number required for TechNova Solutions Ltd donations)
- Organizations that align with TechNova's focus areas: STEM education, workforce development in tech, digital equity, and environmental sustainability

TechNova does **not** make contributions to:
- Political campaigns or PACs
- Religious organizations (unless operating secular programs)
- Individuals
- Organizations with unresolved controversy or ESG concerns

## Approval Process for Corporate Donations

| Donation Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $2,500 | CFO (David Park) |
| $2,500 – $9,999 | CFO + CEO (Sarah Chen) |
| $10,000 and above | CFO + CEO + Board Notification |

All donation requests must be submitted via the Finance portal at least **15 business days** before the intended donation date to allow for IRS verification and approval routing.

---

## Employee Gift Matching Program

TechNova Solutions matches employee charitable donations at a **1:1 ratio** up to **$1,000 per employee per calendar year**:

- Employee makes a personal donation to an eligible 501(c)(3) organization.
- Employee submits a match request via the Finance portal with the donation receipt and organization EIN.
- Finance verifies the organization's 501(c)(3) status.
- TechNova issues the matching donation directly to the organization within **30 days**.
- Match is applied to the current-year matching budget (first-come, first-served until the $100,000 pool is exhausted).

---

## In-Kind Donations

TechNova may donate FlowEngine platform licenses or professional services to qualifying nonprofits:

- In-kind donations are valued at TechNova's standard commercial rate for revenue recognition purposes.
- FlowEngine licenses donated to nonprofits are logged in the [Subscription Management](subscription-management.md) registry under the "Philanthropy" tag.
- Deloitte reviews material in-kind donations as part of the annual audit.

---

## Tax Deductibility

Corporate charitable contributions are deductible up to **10% of taxable income** (US). Finance tracks all contributions in NetSuite and provides a year-end donation summary to Deloitte Tax for inclusion in the Form 1120. Acknowledgment letters from recipient organizations must be obtained for all donations over $250.

---

## Employee-Initiated Donations on Corporate Cards

Employees may not use corporate Brex cards to make charitable donations on TechNova's behalf unless pre-approved by the CFO. Unauthorized charitable charges will be reversed and the employee may be required to reimburse the company. See [Corporate Card Policy](corporate-card-policy.md).

---

## Reporting

Finance includes a quarterly **Corporate Giving Summary** in the Board Package, covering:
- Total donations year-to-date vs. budget
- Recipient organizations and amounts
- Employee gift matching utilization
- In-kind donations issued

The summary is included in the CFO section of the quarterly Board Package (see [Financial Reporting](financial-reporting.md)).
