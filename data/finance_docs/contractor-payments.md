## Overview

TechNova Solutions engages independent contractors for specialized project work that complements our ~500 full-time employees. All contractor engagements and payments are governed by this policy to ensure proper classification, timely payment, and tax compliance. Contractor payments are processed through **Bill.com** with payment terms of **Net-30**.

---

## Contractor Classification

Before engaging a contractor, the hiring manager must verify proper classification. TechNova follows the **IRS Common Law Test** and California **AB5** (for CA-based contractors). Key criteria:

| Factor | Employee Indicator | Contractor Indicator |
|---|---|---|
| Control over work | Company directs how work is done | Contractor controls methods |
| Integration into business | Core business functions | Specialized, project-based |
| Equipment | Company-provided | Contractor uses own tools |
| Permanency | Indefinite | Project-specific term |
| Exclusivity | Works only for TechNova | Works for multiple clients |

Misclassification carries significant legal and tax risk. When in doubt, consult HR (Lisa Thompson, VP HR) and Legal before engaging.

## Engagement Prerequisites

Before a contractor may begin work or be paid:

1. **Signed contract:** TechNova's standard Independent Contractor Agreement (ICA) or vendor MSA, reviewed by Legal.
2. **Statement of Work (SOW):** Defines deliverables, timeline, and total contract value.
3. **W-9 or W-8BEN:** Collected before first payment. Foreign contractors must provide W-8BEN-E.
4. **Vendor onboarding in Bill.com:** Finance sets up ACH details or wire instructions.
5. **Background check:** Required for contractors with access to customer data or production systems (coordinated by HR).
6. **Purchase Requisition approved:** All contractor engagements require an approved PR per [Purchase Requisition](purchase-requisition.md).

## Payment Terms and Process

All contractors are paid on **Net-30** from the date the invoice is received and accepted by Finance.

### Invoicing Requirements

Contractor invoices must include:
- TechNova Solutions as the client entity (or TechNova Solutions Ltd for UK engagements)
- SOW or contract number
- Invoice date and unique invoice number
- Itemized services rendered and period covered
- Total amount due and currency
- ACH/bank details (or Bill.com portal submission)

**Submission deadline:** Invoices received by the **25th of each month** are targeted for payment in the following Net-30 cycle.

### Payment Method by Geography

| Location | Method | Settlement |
|---|---|---|
| US-based contractors | ACH via Bill.com | 1–3 business days |
| UK-based contractors | International wire via SVB | 3–5 business days |
| Canada-based contractors | ACH (CAD) or wire | 3–5 business days |
| Other international | Wire via SVB | 5–7 business days |

## 1099 Reporting

TechNova Solutions issues **Form 1099-NEC** by January 31 for any US-based contractor or sole proprietor receiving more than **$600** in a calendar year. Finance compiles contractor tax data from Bill.com and Gusto (for contractors on Gusto payroll module). See [Tax Compliance](tax-compliance.md) for full details.

## Contractor Spend Tracking

Finance tracks all active contractor engagements in NetSuite, coded to the relevant cost center (see [Cost Center Codes](cost-center-codes.md)). The Finance Controller produces a quarterly **Contractor Spend Report** for the CFO and relevant VPs, showing:

- Active contractors and contract values
- YTD spend by cost center
- Contracts nearing expiry
- Flagged reclassification risks

## FAQ

**Q: Can I start using a contractor before the ICA is signed?**
A: No. Work cannot begin before a signed contract is in place. Urgency is not an exception — contact Legal for a rush review (typically 48-hour turnaround for standard agreements).

**Q: What if a contractor wants to invoice more than the SOW value?**
A: A change order or amended SOW is required, with the same approval thresholds as a new contract. Finance will not pay invoices that exceed the approved PO value without an approved amendment.

**Q: How do I renew a contractor engagement?**
A: Submit a new PR with an updated SOW at least **10 business days** before the current contract expires.
