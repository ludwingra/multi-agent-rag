# Corporate Card Policy

**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Version:** 2.5

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions issues **Brex corporate cards** (issued through SVB/Brex) to eligible employees to facilitate business spending without requiring personal out-of-pocket expense. This policy governs card eligibility, spending limits, acceptable use, and compliance requirements.

---

## Card Eligibility

Corporate cards are issued to employees who meet one or more of the following criteria:

- Director-level and above (all departments)
- Sales Account Executives and Sales Managers
- Employees who travel at least **twice per quarter** for business
- Engineers with approved recurring software/tool budgets

Eligibility is determined by the employee's manager and approved by Finance. Submit eligibility requests to finance@technova.com.

---

## Spending Limits by Role

| Role | Monthly Limit |
|---|---|
| Individual Contributor (approved) | $1,500 |
| Manager | $3,000 |
| Director | $7,500 |
| VP | $15,000 |
| CFO / CEO | $50,000 |

Limits may be adjusted temporarily for specific business needs (e.g., conference sponsorship, large client event) with written CFO approval.

---

## Permitted Uses

Corporate cards may be used for:

- Business travel (flights, hotels, ground transportation) — see [Travel Policy](travel-policy.md)
- Client meals and entertainment (with documented attendee list and business purpose)
- Approved software subscriptions (must be registered in [Subscription Management](subscription-management.md))
- Office supplies under $200
- Conference registrations and professional development

---

## Prohibited Uses

The following transactions are strictly prohibited on corporate cards:

- Personal purchases of any kind
- Cash advances
- Alcohol for personal consumption (client entertainment exceptions must be noted)
- Gift cards (except pre-approved employee recognition amounts under $150)
- Political contributions
- Charitable donations not pre-approved by Finance (see [Charitable Donations](charitable-donations.md))
- Purchases in violation of [Subscription Management](subscription-management.md) policy

---

## Reconciliation Requirements

All cardholders must reconcile their Brex card transactions in **Expensify** within **7 days** of each transaction:

1. Log into Expensify (synced with Brex via direct integration).
2. Tag each transaction with the correct cost center code (see [Cost Center Codes](cost-center-codes.md)).
3. Upload receipts for all transactions over $25.
4. Add a brief business justification for each line item.
5. Submit the expense report to your manager for review.

**End-of-month deadline:** All card transactions from the prior month must be fully reconciled by the **5th of the following month.**

---

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Late reconciliation (>7 days) | Warning email from Finance |
| Repeated late reconciliation (3+ months) | Card temporarily suspended |
| Prohibited personal use | Immediate card suspension; reimbursement required within 5 business days |
| Fraudulent use | Card termination, HR investigation, potential legal action |

---

## Lost or Stolen Cards

Report a lost or stolen card immediately:
1. Call Brex 24/7 support: 1-855-55-BREX
2. Notify Finance at finance@technova.com within 1 business day
3. Brex will issue a replacement card within 3–5 business days

---

## Card Cancellation

Corporate cards are canceled upon:
- Employee offboarding (last day of employment)
- Role change that removes eligibility
- Non-compliance per the above table

Finance runs a monthly audit of active cardholders against the current headcount roster.
