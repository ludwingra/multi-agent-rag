# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Version:** 3.2

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions reimburses employees for reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred in the performance of their job duties. All reimbursements must comply with this policy and be submitted within **30 days** of incurring the expense. Late submissions may be denied at manager discretion.

---

## Eligible Expenses

The following categories are eligible for reimbursement:

| Category | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| T&E | Travel and entertainment for business purposes | Requires client/meeting context |
| Software | Business-critical tools not covered by IT | Requires prior approval |
| Hardware | Peripherals and accessories for work | Laptop covered by IT provisioning |
| Professional Services | Certifications, courses, conferences | Up to $1,500/year per employee |
| Facilities | Home office setup (remote employees only) | One-time $500 stipend |

---

## Approval Thresholds

| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Direct Manager |
| $500 – $4,999 | Department Director |
| $5,000 – $24,999 | VP of Finance or relevant VP |
| $25,000 and above | CFO (David Park) |

---

## Submission Process

1. Log into **Expensify** (app.expensify.com) using your TechNova SSO credentials.
2. Create a new expense report, assign the correct **cost center code** (see [Cost Center Codes](cost-center-codes.md)).
3. Attach digital receipts for all expenses over **$25**.
4. Submit the report to your direct manager for approval.
5. Approved reports are processed on the 1st and 15th payroll cycles via Gusto.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal items, clothing, or gym memberships
- Fines, penalties, or traffic violations
- Alcohol (unless part of approved client entertainment — see [Travel Policy](travel-policy.md))
- Personal travel add-ons (upgrades paid for personal preference, side trips)
- Expenses submitted more than 30 days after the transaction date
- Expenses without receipts over $25

---

## Corporate Cards

Employees with Brex corporate cards should charge business expenses directly to the card rather than seeking personal reimbursement. Corporate card holders must still submit itemized reports in Expensify. See [Corporate Card Policy](corporate-card-policy.md) for card limits and compliance requirements.

---

## International Expenses

Expenses incurred abroad (e.g., at TechNova's London UK office — TechNova Solutions Ltd) are reimbursed in USD at the exchange rate on the date of purchase, as reported by the Expensify integration with XE.com.

## FAQ

**Q: What if I lost a receipt?**
A: Submit a "Missing Receipt Affidavit" in Expensify for amounts under $75. For amounts $75 or more, contact finance@technova.com to review on a case-by-case basis.

**Q: How long does reimbursement take?**
A: Once approved, reimbursements are included in the next Gusto payroll cycle (1st or 15th of the month).

**Q: Can I expense a meal with a colleague?**
A: Yes, for legitimate business discussions. List all attendees and the business purpose in Expensify. Meals with no external client must still have a documented business purpose.

**Q: Who do I contact for policy questions?**
A: Email the Finance team at finance@technova.com or Slack `#finance-help`.
