**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Version:** 2.0

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions produces financial reports at multiple cadences to support management decision-making, investor obligations, and regulatory compliance. All financial reporting is grounded in data from NetSuite ERP, Stripe (billing), Gusto (payroll), and Bill.com (AP).

---

## Financial Statements

TechNova Solutions prepares three core financial statements in accordance with **US GAAP**:

| Statement | Description | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Income Statement (P&L) | Revenue, COGS, gross profit, operating expenses, EBITDA | Monthly, Quarterly, Annual |
| Balance Sheet | Assets, liabilities, shareholders' equity | Monthly, Quarterly, Annual |
| Cash Flow Statement | Operating, investing, financing cash flows | Monthly, Quarterly, Annual |

Annual audited financials are prepared by Deloitte (see [Audit Procedures](audit-procedures.md)).

## Revenue Reporting

Revenue is recognized in accordance with **ASC 606** (see [Revenue Recognition](revenue-recognition.md)). The Finance team reconciles Stripe subscription data to NetSuite monthly. Key revenue metrics reported:

- **New ARR** — New logos signed in the period
- **Expansion ARR** — Upsells to existing FlowEngine customers
- **Churned ARR** — Cancellations and downgrades
- **Net New ARR** — New + Expansion − Churned

---

## Cost Center Reporting

All expenses are coded to one of six cost centers. Finance produces cost center P&Ls for each VP monthly. Budget owners are responsible for variance explanations when actuals exceed budget by more than 10%.

See [Cost Center Codes](cost-center-codes.md) for the full cost center mapping and [Budget Allocation](budget-allocation.md) for planning details.

## Segment Reporting

TechNova Solutions reports financial results by geography to satisfy investor requirements:

| Segment | Entity | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| Americas | TechNova Solutions (Delaware C-Corp) | USD |
| EMEA | TechNova Solutions Ltd (UK subsidiary) | GBP / USD equivalent |
| Canada | TechNova Solutions (Toronto branch) | CAD / USD equivalent |

Intercompany transactions between the US entity and UK subsidiary are eliminated in consolidated financials.

---

## Management Representation Letter

At the close of each annual audit, the CFO and CEO sign a **Management Representation Letter** to Deloitte confirming:
- Completeness and accuracy of financial statements
- No undisclosed liabilities or commitments
- Compliance with applicable laws and regulations

---

## Access and Confidentiality

Financial reports are classified as **Confidential – Internal Use Only** unless explicitly approved for external distribution by the CFO. Sharing financial data outside approved channels (e.g., investor data rooms) without CFO approval is a violation of TechNova's information security policy.
