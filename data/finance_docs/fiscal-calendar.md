## Overview

TechNova Solutions operates on a **calendar fiscal year**: January 1 through December 31. This document provides the authoritative schedule of key finance dates, deadlines, and reporting milestones for FY2025.

---

## FY2025 Quarter Schedule

| Quarter | Period | Key Milestones |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | FY2025 budget locked, W-2 distribution (Jan 31) |
| Q2 | Apr 1 – Jun 30 | 10-Q equivalent filing, Series B covenant review |
| Q3 | Jul 1 – Sep 30 | Profitable since Q3 2024 — one-year profitability milestone |
| Q4 | Oct 1 – Dec 31 | FY2026 budget planning, external audit prep |

## Monthly Finance Calendar

Each month follows a repeatable close schedule:

| Business Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Month-end accruals posted in NetSuite |
| Day 3 | Bank reconciliation complete (SVB + Mercury) |
| Day 5 | Accounts receivable (Stripe) reconciliation |
| Day 5 | Corporate card reconciliation deadline (all cardholders) |
| Day 7 | Accounts payable close (Bill.com) |
| Day 10 | Budget vs. actual report distributed to VPs and Directors |
| Day 10 | Flash P&L shared with Executive team |
| Day 15 | Payroll processing (Gusto — 15th cycle) |
| Day 15 | Board investor update (CFO + CEO) |

## Payroll Schedule

TechNova Solutions pays all full-time employees **twice per month** via Gusto:

| Payroll Cycle | Pay Date | Period Covered |
|---|---|---|
| 1st Cycle | 1st of month | 16th–last day of prior month |
| 2nd Cycle | 15th of month | 1st–15th of current month |

If a pay date falls on a weekend or holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day. See [Payroll Schedule](payroll-schedule.md) for full details.
