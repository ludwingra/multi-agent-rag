**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**

**Version:** 2.3

## Overview

TechNova Solutions processes all vendor invoices through **Bill.com**, integrated with our NetSuite ERP. This policy ensures invoices are received, validated, approved, and paid accurately and on time. Proper invoice processing supports our vendor relationships and maintains accurate financial records.

---

## Invoice Receipt Requirements

All vendors must submit invoices electronically to **ap@technova.com** or directly through the Bill.com vendor portal. Paper invoices are discouraged; however, if received, Finance will digitize and upload within 2 business days.

A valid TechNova invoice must include:

- TechNova Solutions legal entity name (or TechNova Solutions Ltd for UK transactions)
- Corresponding **Purchase Order (PO) number** (see [Purchase Requisition](purchase-requisition.md))
- Invoice date and unique invoice number
- Itemized description of goods or services delivered
- Payment terms (default: Net-30)
- Vendor bank details or ACH/wire instructions
- Applicable tax IDs (W-9 for domestic, W-8BEN-E for foreign vendors)

---

## Three-Way Match Process

Finance applies a **three-way match** before approving payment:

1. **Purchase Order** — Issued by Finance via NetSuite after PR approval
2. **Receipt/Delivery Confirmation** — Signed off by the requesting department (goods received or service delivered)
3. **Vendor Invoice** — Matches PO amount within an **acceptable variance of ±$50 or 2%**, whichever is lower

Invoices failing the three-way match are placed on hold and routed back to the department owner for resolution within **5 business days**.

---

## Approval Workflow in Bill.com

| Invoice Amount | Approver in Bill.com |
|---|---|
| Under $5,000 | Department Director |
| $5,000 – $24,999 | VP of relevant cost center |
| $25,000 and above | CFO (David Park) |
| Recurring invoices (approved contract) | Auto-approved by Finance Analyst |

Approvers receive email notifications from Bill.com and must action invoices within **3 business days** to avoid late payments.

---

## Payment Schedule

TechNova Solutions runs two payment cycles per week:

| Cycle | Day | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle A | Tuesday | ACH (domestic) |
| Cycle B | Thursday | ACH + Wire (international) |

Rush payments outside the standard cycle require CFO approval and carry a **$25 processing fee** charged to the requesting cost center.

---

## Credit Memos and Disputes

- Credit memos from vendors are applied to the next outstanding invoice from that vendor.
- Invoice disputes must be raised within **15 days** of invoice receipt. Finance coordinates with the vendor and the requesting department to resolve discrepancies.
- Disputed invoices are not paid until resolution, even if they fall past the due date (the vendor is notified to avoid late fees).

---

## Recurring Subscriptions

Software and SaaS invoices are cross-referenced against the approved subscription registry (see [Subscription Management](subscription-management.md)). Invoices for software not in the registry are held pending confirmation from IT and Finance.

---

## Record Retention

All invoices, POs, and payment confirmations are stored in NetSuite and Bill.com for a minimum of **7 years** to comply with IRS record-keeping requirements and support the annual Deloitte external audit (see [Audit Procedures](audit-procedures.md)).

## FAQ

**Q: What happens if a vendor invoices without a PO number?**
A: Finance will contact the vendor to request the PO number. If the purchase was unauthorized (no PO exists), the invoice is returned to the vendor and the internal requester is notified for remediation.

**Q: Can invoices be paid in currencies other than USD?**
A: Yes, for TechNova Solutions Ltd (UK entity). GBP invoices are paid via international wire through Silicon Valley Bank. All other currencies require CFO approval.

**Q: What is the escalation path for delayed approvals?**
A: Finance sends reminders at Day 2 and Day 3. After Day 3, the invoice is escalated to the department head. Invoices at risk of becoming past-due are flagged in the weekly AP aging report distributed to VPs.
