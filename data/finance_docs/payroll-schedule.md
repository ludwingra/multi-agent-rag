## Overview

TechNova Solutions processes payroll **twice per month** via **Gusto** for all full-time and part-time employees in the United States and Canada. UK employees under TechNova Solutions Ltd are paid monthly via BACS transfer, managed by Mazars LLP. This document covers the 2025 payroll schedule, processing deadlines, and employee responsibilities.

---

## 2025 US Payroll Schedule (Gusto)

| Pay Cycle | Pay Date | Period Covered | Payroll Submitted By |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan Cycle 1 | January 15 | Jan 1 – Jan 15 | January 13 |
| Jan Cycle 2 | February 1 | Jan 16 – Jan 31 | January 28 |
| Feb Cycle 1 | February 15 | Feb 1 – Feb 15 | February 12 |
| Feb Cycle 2 | March 1 | Feb 16 – Feb 28 | February 26 |
| Mar Cycle 1 | March 15 | Mar 1 – Mar 15 | March 13 |
| Mar Cycle 2 | April 1 | Mar 16 – Mar 31 | March 28 |
| Q2–Q4 | Continues pattern | 1st/15th of each month | 2 business days before pay date |

**Rule:** If a pay date falls on a weekend or federal holiday, payment is made on the **preceding business day**.

## Payroll Processing Deadlines

| Task | Deadline | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| New hire setup in Gusto | 3 business days before first pay date | HR |
| Salary/compensation changes | 5 business days before affected pay date | HR + Finance |
| Termination processing | Same day as last day | HR |
| Bonus payroll (off-cycle) | 5 business days prior notice to Finance | Manager + HR |
| Expense reimbursements via payroll | Approved in Expensify by Day -3 | Employee + Manager |

## Pay Components

TechNova Solutions payroll includes:

| Component | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Base Salary | Regular | Annual salary divided by 24 pay periods |
| Variable Compensation | Bonus | Sales commissions paid monthly; performance bonuses quarterly |
| Expense Reimbursements | Non-taxable | Approved Expensify reports (see [Expense Reimbursement Policy](expense-reimbursement.md)) |
| 401(k) Contribution | Pre-tax deduction | Up to IRS annual limit ($23,000 in 2025) |
| 401(k) Company Match | Employer contribution | 4% of base salary, immediate vesting (Fidelity) |
| Health Insurance | Pre-tax deduction | Employee share; TechNova covers 80% of premium |
| FSA / HSA | Pre-tax deduction | Employee-elected amount |
| State/Federal Income Tax | Withholding | Based on W-4 on file in Gusto |
| FICA (SS + Medicare) | Withholding | 7.65% employee share |

## Off-Cycle Payroll

Off-cycle payroll runs (e.g., correction runs, spot bonuses) must be requested at least **5 business days** in advance through HR. Off-cycle runs require:

- Manager approval
- HR sign-off
- Finance Controller confirmation
- CFO approval if total off-cycle amount exceeds $50,000

Off-cycle payroll is processed as a direct deposit via Gusto and may take 1–2 additional business days to settle.

---

## Canadian Payroll (Toronto Support Center)

Toronto employees are paid in CAD on the same 1st/15th cycle via a separate Gusto Canada entity. Payroll taxes follow CRA guidelines including CPP and EI deductions. T4 slips are issued by **February 28** each year.

---

## UK Payroll (TechNova Solutions Ltd)

London employees are paid monthly on the **last business day of the month** via BACS. UK payroll is processed by Mazars LLP and includes PAYE, National Insurance contributions, and pension auto-enrollment. P60s are issued by **May 31** each year.

---

## Direct Deposit and Pay Stubs

- All US employees are required to enroll in direct deposit via Gusto self-service.
- Pay stubs are available in the Gusto employee portal on the pay date.
- Employees without direct deposit enrolled will receive a paper check — Finance strongly discourages this option due to processing delays.

---

## Payroll Questions and Corrections

Contact HR at hr@technova.com or the Gusto employee support line for pay stub questions. For corrections (wrong amount, missing reimbursement), submit a ticket to hr@technova.com with the affected pay period and description. Finance will process corrections in the next available payroll cycle.

See [Tax Compliance](tax-compliance.md) for information on W-2 distribution and withholding requirements.
