**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Overview

TechNova Solutions maintains small petty cash funds at each office location to cover minor, incidental business expenses that are impractical to process through the standard purchase requisition or expense reimbursement workflow. This policy governs the establishment, use, custodianship, and replenishment of petty cash funds.

---

## Petty Cash Fund Limits by Location

| Office | Fund Limit | Custodian Role |
|---|---|---|
| San Francisco, CA (HQ) | $500 | Office Manager |
| Austin, TX (Engineering Hub) | $300 | Site Lead |
| London, UK (Sales Office) | £250 | Office Coordinator |
| Toronto, Canada (Support Center) | CAD $300 | Site Manager |

Funds are kept in a locked cash box accessible only to the designated custodian and their designated backup.

---

## Eligible Petty Cash Purchases

Petty cash may be used for:

- Office supplies under $50 (stamps, pens, labels)
- Refreshments for in-office team meetings under $75
- Courier/delivery fees under $30
- Minor emergency repairs or replacements under $50
- Parking validation for clients visiting the office

---

## Prohibited Uses

- Any purchase over $100 (use [Expense Reimbursement Policy](expense-reimbursement.md) instead)
- Personal purchases of any kind
- Advances to employees (payroll advances must go through Gusto via HR)
- Purchases that should go through a Purchase Requisition (see [Purchase Requisition](purchase-requisition.md))
- Recurring expenses of any amount

---

## Petty Cash Voucher Process

Every petty cash disbursement must be documented with a **Petty Cash Voucher**:

1. Obtain a Petty Cash Voucher form from the Finance shared drive.
2. Fill in: date, amount, payee, business purpose, cost center code (see [Cost Center Codes](cost-center-codes.md)).
3. Attach the original receipt.
4. Custodian signs and issues cash.
5. Recipient countersigns the voucher.

At all times: **cash + vouchers + receipts = fund limit**. Discrepancies must be reported to Finance immediately.

---

## Fund Replenishment

When the fund balance falls below **25% of the fund limit**, the custodian submits a replenishment request:

1. Compile all petty cash vouchers and receipts into a replenishment packet.
2. Categorize expenses by cost center code.
3. Submit to the local site's manager for approval (under $100 threshold applies).
4. Finance issues a check or ACH reimbursement to the fund within **3 business days**.
5. Custodian deposits the funds and reconciles to restore the fund to its full limit.

---

## Monthly Reconciliation

Petty cash custodians must reconcile the fund at the **end of each month**:

- Count physical cash on hand.
- Total all outstanding vouchers and receipts.
- Confirm sum equals the fund limit.
- Submit the monthly reconciliation form to Finance by the **5th of the following month**.

Finance conducts a surprise audit of petty cash funds at least **twice per year** at each location.

---

## Custodian Responsibilities

The custodian is personally responsible for the integrity of the petty cash fund. Shortages that cannot be explained by legitimate business expenses will be investigated by Finance. Repeated unexplained shortages may result in disciplinary action.

Custodian changes require Finance notification and a formal count/transfer with both outgoing and incoming custodians present.

---

## FAQ

**Q: What if I need more than $100 in cash quickly?**
A: Use your corporate card if you have one (see [Corporate Card Policy](corporate-card-policy.md)) or submit an expense reimbursement. Petty cash is not intended for large or urgent purchases.

**Q: Can I reimburse myself from petty cash?**
A: Yes, for eligible purchases under $100. Complete a petty cash voucher with the original receipt.
