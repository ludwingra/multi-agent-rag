# Purchase Requisition Policy

**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Version:** 2.1

---

## Overview

A Purchase Requisition (PR) is the formal internal process by which TechNova Solutions employees request authorization to purchase goods or services on behalf of the company. All purchases above **$500** that are not on an active vendor contract require a Purchase Requisition before a Purchase Order (PO) is issued.

---

## When to Submit a Purchase Requisition

A PR is required for:

- New vendor relationships or one-time services over $500
- Software subscriptions not already in the approved [Subscription Management](subscription-management.md) registry
- Hardware purchases (servers, equipment, peripherals) over $500
- Professional services engagements (consultants, agencies, legal)
- Any purchase not covered by an existing blanket PO

A PR is **not** required for:
- Individual expense reimbursements (use [Expense Reimbursement Policy](expense-reimbursement.md))
- Recurring invoices from approved vendors under an active PO
- Petty cash purchases under $100 (see [Petty Cash Policy](petty-cash-policy.md))

---

## Approval Thresholds

| Purchase Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| $500 – $4,999 | Department Director |
| $5,000 – $24,999 | VP of relevant department |
| $25,000 and above | CFO (David Park) |
| Multi-year contracts (any value) | CFO + Legal review |

---

## How to Submit a Purchase Requisition

1. Navigate to the Finance portal in the company intranet.
2. Complete the **PR Form** with the following fields:
   - Vendor name, address, and contact
   - Description and business justification
   - Itemized list of goods/services and unit prices
   - Total cost and currency
   - Requested delivery/start date
   - **Cost center code** (see [Cost Center Codes](cost-center-codes.md))
   - Budget line item reference
3. Attach any quotes, SOWs, or vendor proposals.
4. Submit for routing to the appropriate approver per the threshold table above.

---

## Review Criteria

Finance will evaluate PRs against the following criteria:

- **Budget availability:** Is there approved budget in the relevant cost center for the current fiscal quarter?
- **Vendor due diligence:** Is the vendor verified in TechNova's approved vendor list? New vendors must complete a vendor onboarding form and W-9/W-8.
- **Security review:** Software purchases are forwarded to the IT Security team for a 3-day review window.
- **Legal review:** Contracts over $25,000 or multi-year agreements are routed to Legal (external counsel, Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati).

---

## After Approval

1. Finance issues a **Purchase Order (PO)** number via NetSuite ERP.
2. The PO number must be referenced on all vendor invoices.
3. Invoices are processed through **Bill.com** per [Invoice Processing](invoice-processing.md).
4. Payment terms default to **Net-30** unless otherwise negotiated (see [Vendor Payment Terms](vendor-payment-terms.md)).

---

## Rejection and Appeals

Rejected PRs will include written justification. The requester may revise and resubmit or escalate to the CFO if they believe the rejection was in error. Appeals must be submitted within **10 business days** of the rejection notice.

---

## FAQ

**Q: Can I order something before the PR is approved?**
A: No. Purchases made before PR approval may not be reimbursed and may result in disciplinary action.

**Q: How long does PR approval take?**
A: Typically 2–5 business days depending on the approval tier. CFO-level approvals may take up to 7 business days.

**Q: What if I need something urgently?**
A: Email finance@technova.com with "URGENT PR" in the subject and include full justification. Emergency approvals require VP authorization.
