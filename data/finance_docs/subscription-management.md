## Overview

TechNova Solutions uses a broad portfolio of SaaS tools to run its business and FlowEngine product. This policy establishes a centralized **Software Subscription Registry** to ensure all subscriptions are approved, tracked, and renewed or cancelled deliberately. Unmanaged subscriptions represent a significant source of financial waste and security risk.

---

## Subscription Registry

All software subscriptions must be registered in the **Software Subscription Registry** maintained in the Finance SharePoint. The registry captures:

- Tool name and vendor
- Business owner (department + named individual)
- Annual cost and billing cycle
- Cost center code
- Renewal date
- Approval status

The registry is reviewed quarterly by Finance and IT, and reconciled against Bill.com and Brex card charges.

## Adding a New Subscription

1. Check the Software Subscription Registry to confirm the tool is not already available company-wide.
2. Submit a request via the Finance portal with: tool name, business justification, estimated cost, and cost center.
3. IT Security reviews all new software for data privacy and security compliance (3-business-day SLA).
4. Finance approves based on budget availability (see [Budget Allocation](budget-allocation.md)).
5. Once approved, the tool is added to the registry and billing is set up via the corporate card or ACH per [Invoice Processing](invoice-processing.md).

---

## Renewal Management

Finance sends renewal alerts to the business owner **60 days before** the renewal date for subscriptions over $5,000. The business owner must confirm renewal or initiate cancellation within **30 days** of the alert. Non-response defaults to a **hold** (not auto-renewal) for subscriptions over $10,000.

---

## Unauthorized Subscriptions

Employees who sign up for software using personal cards or company cards without registry approval:

1. Will be asked to immediately cancel or transfer the subscription.
2. May not be reimbursed for the expense.
3. Are subject to disciplinary action for repeated violations.

Finance audits Brex card charges monthly for unregistered software vendors and follows up with cardholders directly.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I try a free tier of a tool without going through this process?**
A: Yes, for personal use or small evaluations with no company data. As soon as company data (customer info, code, financials) is involved, the tool must be registered regardless of cost.

**Q: How do I cancel a subscription?**
A: Update the registry to "Pending Cancellation" status, ensure the vendor is notified before the renewal date, and confirm cancellation with Finance. Finance will remove the billing method from the vendor's system.
