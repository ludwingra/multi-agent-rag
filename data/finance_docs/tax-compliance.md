## Overview

TechNova Solutions is incorporated as a **Delaware C-Corporation** (EIN: on file with Finance). The company has a UK subsidiary, **TechNova Solutions Ltd**, registered in England and Wales. This policy outlines TechNova's obligations and procedures for federal, state, local, and international tax compliance.

---

## US Federal Tax

| Filing | Form | Due Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate Income Tax | Form 1120 | April 15 (or Oct 15 with extension) | External filing via Deloitte Tax |
| Estimated Quarterly Taxes | Form 1120-W | Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Dec 15 | Finance calculates and remits |
| Employer FICA / FUTA | Form 941 / 940 | Quarterly / Annual | Via Gusto payroll integration |
| Independent Contractor | 1099-NEC | January 31 | Via Gusto for US contractors |
| Foreign Vendor Withholding | Form 1042-S | March 15 | W-8 collection required |

## State and Local Tax

TechNova Solutions has **nexus** in the following states and is subject to state corporate income tax and sales tax obligations:

| State | Nexus Basis | Tax Types |
|---|---|---|
| California | HQ (San Francisco, CA) | Corporate income, payroll, sales tax |
| Texas | Engineering hub (Austin, TX) | Payroll (no state income tax) |
| New York | Remote employees | Payroll withholding |
| Other states | >$100K revenue or 200 transactions | Economic nexus — sales tax via Avalara |

Sales tax on FlowEngine SaaS subscriptions is calculated and remitted automatically via the **Avalara** integration with Stripe. Finance reviews Avalara filings monthly.

## Delaware Franchise Tax

As a Delaware C-Corp, TechNova Solutions owes Delaware Franchise Tax annually, due **March 1**. Finance uses the **Assumed Par Value Capital** method for calculation, which is typically more favorable than the Authorized Shares method given TechNova's capital structure (managed via Carta).

---

## R&D Tax Credits

TechNova Solutions claims the **Federal R&D Tax Credit (Section 41)** annually, reflecting qualifying research activities on the FlowEngine platform. R&D credit analysis is conducted by Deloitte Tax in conjunction with the annual tax return. The R&D expense documentation is maintained by the Engineering team (VP: Raj Patel) in coordination with Finance.

---

## Transfer Pricing

Intercompany transactions between TechNova Solutions (US) and TechNova Solutions Ltd (UK) are governed by a **Transfer Pricing Policy** prepared by Deloitte and reviewed annually. The current intercompany markup on shared services is **8%**. Documentation is maintained per OECD guidelines and US Treasury Regulation §1.482.

---

## Employee Tax Obligations

- **W-2s** are issued by Gusto no later than January 31 for all employees.
- **W-4 updates**: Employees must submit updated W-4 forms to Gusto when their withholding status changes.
- **Stock options**: Tax implications for ISO and NSO option exercises are communicated by Finance and Carta to affected employees before exercise.
- **401(k)**: Contributions are pre-tax (traditional) or post-tax (Roth); tax treatment reflected automatically in Gusto.

See [Payroll Schedule](payroll-schedule.md) for payroll processing dates.

## FAQ

**Q: How does TechNova handle sales tax on FlowEngine subscriptions?**
A: TechNova uses Avalara integrated with Stripe to automatically calculate and remit sales tax in applicable states. Customers see itemized tax on their invoices.

**Q: What happens if I receive equity from TechNova?**
A: Finance and Carta will send a notice regarding the tax implications of your equity grant. Consult a personal tax advisor for individual guidance; TechNova does not provide individual tax advice.

**Q: Who manages TechNova's tax filings?**
A: Deloitte Tax manages federal and California filings. Mazars LLP handles UK filings. Finance (CFO David Park) coordinates all filings.
