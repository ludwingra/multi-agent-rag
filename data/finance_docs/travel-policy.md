# Travel and Entertainment Policy

**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Version:** 2.4

---

## Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for business travel and entertainment at TechNova Solutions to ensure cost-effective, safe, and consistent management of travel expenses while supporting our mission of delivering the FlowEngine platform to enterprise clients worldwide.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees, contractors, and authorized guests traveling on behalf of TechNova Solutions. Offices covered include:

- **HQ:** San Francisco, CA
- **Engineering Hub:** Austin, TX
- **Sales Office:** London, UK
- **Support Center:** Toronto, Canada

---

## Pre-Travel Approval

All business travel requiring airfare or hotel must be **pre-approved** before booking:

| Trip Cost | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Direct Manager |
| $1,000 – $4,999 | Director |
| $5,000 – $24,999 | VP |
| $25,000+ | CFO (David Park) |

Pre-approval requests should be submitted via the Finance intake form in Slack `#travel-requests` at least **5 business days** before departure.

---

## Airfare

- **Domestic flights:** Economy class required for all flights under 4 hours. Premium Economy permitted for flights over 4 hours with Director approval.
- **International flights:** Economy class required. Business class permitted for flights over 8 hours with VP approval.
- Book at least **14 days in advance** to minimize cost. Last-minute bookings require Director sign-off.
- Use the TechNova preferred booking portal (Navan) or book directly through the airline and expense via Expensify.

---

## Hotel Accommodations

| City | Nightly Rate Cap |
|---|---|
| San Francisco, CA | $275 |
| Austin, TX | $200 |
| London, UK | £225 (~$285) |
| Toronto, Canada | CAD $250 (~$185) |
| Other domestic cities | $200 |
| Other international cities | $250 |

Exceptions require Director approval and must be documented in Expensify with a business justification.

---

## Meals and Per Diem

Employees on overnight business trips may expense meals up to:

| Meal | Per Diem |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | $20 |
| Lunch | $35 |
| Dinner | $75 |
| Daily maximum | $130 |

Team or client dinners: up to **$125 per person** with documented attendee list and business purpose. Alcohol is permitted at client entertainment events (reasonable amounts) — categorize as "Client Entertainment" in Expensify.

---

## Ground Transportation

- Use Uber, Lyft, or equivalent — always select standard (not Uber Black unless arrival/departure timing requires it).
- Rental cars require pre-approval. Use economy or compact class. TechNova's insurance covers rental collisions; decline the rental company CDW.
- Personal vehicle mileage: reimbursed at **$0.67/mile** (2025 IRS rate). Log mileage in Expensify.

---

## Entertainment

Client entertainment must be tied to a specific business opportunity or relationship. Document:
- Names and companies of all attendees
- Business purpose
- Amount spent

Entertainment expenses over **$500 per event** require VP pre-approval.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Travel Expenses

- Travel insurance (covered by company policy)
- Personal entertainment (movies, spas, personal excursions)
- Flight upgrades beyond policy class
- Parking tickets or traffic fines
- Expenses for spouses or companions unless pre-approved

---

## Submission

Submit all travel expenses within **30 days** of return via Expensify. Assign cost center codes per [Cost Center Codes](cost-center-codes.md). See also [Expense Reimbursement Policy](expense-reimbursement.md) for general submission procedures.
