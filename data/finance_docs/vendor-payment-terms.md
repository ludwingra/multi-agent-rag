# Vendor Payment Terms Policy

**TechNova Solutions | Finance Department**
**Document Owner:** David Park, CFO
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025
**Version:** 1.6

---

## Overview

This policy defines TechNova Solutions' standard payment terms for all vendors and outlines the process for negotiating non-standard terms. Consistent payment practices support strong vendor relationships, optimize cash flow, and ensure accurate financial reporting in NetSuite.

---

## Standard Payment Terms

| Vendor Type | Standard Terms | Method |
|---|---|---|
| General vendors (domestic) | Net-30 | ACH via Bill.com |
| General vendors (international) | Net-30 | International wire via SVB |
| Contractors (individuals) | Net-30 | ACH via Bill.com |
| Law firms / professional services | Net-30 | ACH or check |
| Cloud providers (AWS, GCP, etc.) | Monthly auto-pay | Credit card (Brex) |
| SaaS subscriptions | Annual prepay or monthly | Credit card (Brex) or ACH |
| Staffing agencies | Net-15 | ACH |

---

## Early Payment Discounts

TechNova Solutions may take advantage of early payment discounts when offered:

- **2/10 Net-30** (2% discount if paid within 10 days): Finance will evaluate based on current cash position. CFO approval required.
- Discounts resulting in savings over **$500** should always be pursued subject to cash availability.
- Finance tracks early pay discount opportunities in the Bill.com AP dashboard.

---

## Late Payment and Penalties

TechNova Solutions is committed to paying all vendors on time. If a payment is late:

- Finance will contact the vendor proactively and request a waiver of late fees.
- Late fees are charged to the requesting department's cost center as an overage.
- Vendors may place accounts on hold after **15 days past due** — this must be escalated to the CFO immediately.

---

## Requesting Non-Standard Terms

Non-standard payment terms (e.g., Net-45, Net-60, partial milestones) may be negotiated on a case-by-case basis:

1. The department requesting the vendor relationship initiates the request via the Finance portal.
2. Finance evaluates the cash flow impact and vendor risk profile.
3. Approval levels:
   - Net-45 or longer: Director approval
   - Net-60 or longer: VP approval
   - Milestone-based payments: CFO approval + Legal review

All non-standard terms must be documented in the executed contract and reflected in the NetSuite vendor record.

## New Vendor Onboarding

Before a new vendor can be paid, they must complete the vendor onboarding process:

1. Submit a completed **W-9** (domestic) or **W-8BEN-E** (foreign) to ap@technova.com.
2. Provide bank details for ACH setup in Bill.com.
3. Sign TechNova's standard **vendor agreement** or provide their own MSA for Legal review.
4. For vendors over $50,000/year, Finance conducts a brief financial health check.

Onboarding typically takes **3–5 business days**. Rush onboarding is available with CFO approval.

---

## Contractor Payment Terms

Independent contractors are paid on **Net-30** terms from invoice receipt. Contractors must:

- Submit invoices via Bill.com contractor portal or to ap@technova.com
- Reference the applicable Statement of Work (SOW) or contract number
- Submit invoices by the **25th of each month** for payment in the following cycle

See [Contractor Payments](contractor-payments.md) for full contractor billing procedures.

---

## 1099 and Tax Reporting

TechNova Solutions issues **1099-NEC** forms to all US-based contractors and vendors receiving over **$600** in a calendar year. Finance compiles and submits 1099s by **January 31** each year via the Gusto 1099 module. Foreign vendors receiving payments are subject to W-8 withholding requirements (contact finance@technova.com for details).

## FAQ

**Q: What if a vendor says we have the wrong payment terms in our system?**
A: Email ap@technova.com with the executed contract showing the agreed terms. Finance will update NetSuite within 2 business days.

**Q: Can a vendor charge interest on late payments?**
A: Yes, if stated in the vendor contract. TechNova's standard agreement caps interest at 1.5%/month. Negotiate removal of interest clauses for new vendor agreements when possible.
