# Benefits Overview

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions offers a comprehensive benefits package designed to support the health, financial security, and wellbeing of our employees and their families. This document summarizes all available benefits. For full plan details, refer to the provider portals linked in BambooHR.

---

## Health Insurance — Aetna

TechNova partners with **Aetna** to provide medical, dental, and vision coverage.

| Plan Tier | Monthly Premium (Employee) | Monthly Premium (Employee + Family) |
|---|---|---|
| Aetna Select PPO | $0 (fully covered) | $320/month |
| Aetna Choice HDHP | $0 (fully covered) | $180/month |
| Dental (Aetna DMO) | $0 (fully covered) | $45/month |
| Vision (VSP) | $0 (fully covered) | $22/month |

- TechNova covers **100% of the employee-only premium** for all plans.
- Coverage begins on the **first day of employment**.
- Enrollment must be completed within **30 days of start date** via the Aetna portal linked in BambooHR.

---

## Retirement — Fidelity 401(k)

TechNova offers a **401(k) plan administered by Fidelity** with the following features:

- **Company match**: 4% of gross salary (dollar-for-dollar up to 4%)
- **Vesting schedule**: Immediate vesting on employee contributions; 2-year cliff vesting on employer match
- **Contribution limits**: Up to IRS limits ($23,000 in 2025; $30,500 if age 50+)
- Employees can enroll at any time via the Fidelity NetBenefits portal

---

## Equity — Carta

All full-time employees are eligible for **equity compensation** in the form of Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) or Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) depending on role level.

- Equity grants are managed on **Carta**
- Standard vesting schedule: **4-year vesting with 1-year cliff**
- Details vary by role; see the [Stock Options Guide](stock-options-guide.md) for full information

---

## Parental Leave

TechNova provides **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** for all parents (birth, adoption, or foster placement), gender-neutral. See the [Parental Leave Policy](parental-leave-policy.md) for eligibility and process details.

## Mental Health

- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP)**: 8 free counseling sessions per year via Spring Health
- **Mental health days**: Up to 3 paid mental health days per year (separate from PTO)
- See the [Mental Health Resources](mental-health-resources.md) document for full details

## Open Enrollment

Open enrollment takes place every year in **November** for coverage effective January 1. Employees will receive reminders from HR via Slack (#people-ops) and BambooHR at least 4 weeks before the deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When does my health coverage start?**
A: Coverage starts on your first day. You must enroll within 30 days via BambooHR.

**Q: Can I add a domestic partner to my health plan?**
A: Yes. Aetna recognizes domestic partners. Documentation (e.g., affidavit of domestic partnership) is required.

**Q: How do I access my equity information?**
A: Log in to Carta using your TechNova email. Contact hr@technovasolutions.com if you need access.
