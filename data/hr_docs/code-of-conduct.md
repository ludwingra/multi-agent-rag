# Code of Conduct

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Purpose

TechNova Solutions is committed to maintaining a workplace that is respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment and discrimination. This Code of Conduct applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and anyone acting on behalf of TechNova Solutions, regardless of location or role.

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. See the [Termination Process](termination-process.md) for details.

---

## Core Values

TechNova's four core values guide every interaction, decision, and behavior:

1. **Innovation** — Challenge the status quo and think creatively to solve problems
2. **Transparency** — Communicate openly, share information proactively, and act with integrity
3. **Collaboration** — Work across teams and time zones as one unified team
4. **Customer Obsession** — Make decisions with the customer's success at the center

---

## Professional Standards

All TechNova employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with dignity and respect
- Communicate professionally in all channels (Slack, Jira, GitHub, email, video calls)
- Meet deadlines, communicate proactively when blockers arise, and own their commitments
- Protect confidential company and customer data (see Data Privacy section below)
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose any potential conflicts to HR promptly
- Not engage in moonlighting or outside employment that directly competes with TechNova without written approval

---

## Harassment and Discrimination

TechNova has a **zero-tolerance policy** for harassment and discrimination of any kind, including but not limited to:

- Verbal, written, or physical harassment
- Sexual harassment or unwanted advances
- Discrimination based on race, color, national origin, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic
- Bullying, intimidation, or retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith

This policy applies to all work-related settings, including remote environments, Slack channels, team offsites, and company-sponsored events.

---

## Reporting Violations

Employees who experience or witness a violation of this Code should:

1. Report the incident via the **anonymous ethics hotline** at ethics.technovasolutions.com, OR
2. Contact HR directly at hr@technovasolutions.com, OR
3. Speak with their manager or VP HR Lisa Thompson

All reports are treated with confidentiality to the extent permitted by law. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

For formal grievance procedures, refer to the [Grievance Procedure](grievance-procedure.md).

---

## Data Privacy and Confidentiality

Employees handle sensitive data belonging to TechNova customers and partners. All employees must:

- Access only data necessary for their role
- Never share credentials or access tokens
- Report suspected data breaches immediately to security@technovasolutions.com
- Comply with all applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA)
- Sign and adhere to the Confidentiality and IP Assignment Agreement (provided at hire)

---

## Social Media

Employees may use social media personally but must:

- Never share confidential product roadmap, financial data, or customer information
- Not make statements that could be construed as official TechNova positions without authorization
- Be respectful of colleagues; the same anti-harassment standards apply online

---

## Gifts and Entertainment

- Employees may accept gifts of up to **$50 in value** from vendors or customers
- Gifts exceeding $50 must be reported to HR and may need to be declined
- Never accept cash or cash equivalents from any external party

---

## Acknowledgment

All employees are required to review and acknowledge this Code of Conduct annually via BambooHR. New hires complete acknowledgment during Week 1 onboarding. See the [Onboarding Guide](onboarding-guide.md) for the full new-hire checklist.
