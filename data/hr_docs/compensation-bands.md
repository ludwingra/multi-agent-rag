# Compensation Bands

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson | Confidential

---

## Overview

This document defines TechNova Solutions' compensation philosophy and salary bands for all full-time roles. Compensation at TechNova is designed to be **competitive with the top 65th percentile of the San Francisco Bay Area market**, benchmarked annually using Radford and Levels.fyi data.

Total compensation at TechNova consists of:

1. **Base salary**
2. **Equity** (ISOs or RSUs — see [Stock Options Guide](stock-options-guide.md))
3. **Discretionary annual bonus** (5–15% of base salary depending on level and performance)
4. **Benefits** (see [Benefits Overview](benefits-overview.md))

TechNova applies **geo-based pay adjustments** for roles filled outside the San Francisco Bay Area:

| Location | Adjustment Factor |
|---|---|
| San Francisco Bay Area, CA | 1.00 (baseline) |
| Austin, TX | 0.88 |
| London, UK (in GBP, local market) | Local benchmark |
| Toronto, CA (in CAD, local market) | Local benchmark |
| Other US remote locations (Tier 2) | 0.85 – 0.92 |
| Other US remote locations (Tier 3) | 0.78 – 0.85 |

## Annual Merit Increases

In conjunction with the Year-End performance review cycle (December), TechNova conducts an **annual merit review**:

- Employees rated "Meets Expectations" (3): ~3–4% merit increase
- Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" (4): ~5–7% merit increase
- Employees rated "Exceptional" (5): ~8–12% merit increase
- Increases take effect February 1 of the following year

See the [Performance Review Process](performance-review-process.md) for rating criteria.

---

## Promotion Compensation Adjustment

Employees promoted to a higher level receive a salary adjustment to at least the **midpoint of the new band** or a minimum 10% increase, whichever is greater. Promotions are announced with the Year-End review results.

---

## Confidentiality

Compensation information is confidential. Employees are prohibited from disclosing their compensation to external parties. Internal pay transparency between colleagues is permitted within the bounds of the National Labor Relations Act (NLRA).
