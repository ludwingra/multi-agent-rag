# Diversity & Inclusion Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Our Commitment

TechNova Solutions is committed to building and sustaining a diverse, equitable, and inclusive workplace where every employee — regardless of background, identity, or experience — can thrive, be heard, and contribute meaningfully to our mission.

We recognize that diverse teams build better products. FlowEngine, our enterprise workflow automation platform, serves customers across industries and geographies. Our team must reflect the breadth of the world we serve.

## Inclusive Hiring Practices

TechNova holds all recruiting processes to the following standards:

- **Structured interviews**: All candidates answer the same questions; scoring rubrics prevent subjective bias
- **Diverse interview panels**: At least one underrepresented interviewer on every final-round panel
- **Blind resume screening**: Greenhouse anonymizes names and schools during initial screening
- **Job descriptions**: Reviewed by HR for gendered language before posting
- **Referral equity**: The [Employee Referral Program](employee-referral-program.md) includes outreach to underrepresented communities

## Inclusion Practices in Day-to-Day Work

- **Pronouns**: Employees are encouraged to share pronouns in their Slack profile and Zoom display name. Misgendering is a Code of Conduct violation.
- **Meeting accessibility**: All recurring meetings include closed captions. Meeting notes are shared in Confluence within 24 hours.
- **Religious observances**: Employees may swap company holidays to observe their own religious or cultural holidays. Submit a swap request via BambooHR.
- **Language**: English is the working language at TechNova. Employees are encouraged to speak clearly and avoid idioms that may exclude non-native speakers.

## Training and Accountability

All employees complete the following D&I training annually, assigned in Confluence:
- Unconscious Bias Awareness (1 hour, online)
- Inclusive Leadership for Managers (2 hours, managers only)
- Allyship in the Workplace (1 hour, online)

Completion is tracked by HR. Non-completion by December 31 is flagged in Lattice during the Year-End performance review cycle. Refer to the [Performance Review Process](performance-review-process.md) for how behavioral standards factor into reviews.

---

## Reporting and Accountability

Employees who experience or witness discrimination or exclusion should:
1. Report via the anonymous ethics hotline at ethics.technovasolutions.com
2. Contact HR at hr@technovasolutions.com
3. Review the [Grievance Procedure](grievance-procedure.md) for formal escalation steps

TechNova investigates all reports promptly. Retaliation is strictly prohibited and constitutes a [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md) violation.
