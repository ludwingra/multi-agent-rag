# Dress Code Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions maintains a **relaxed, business-casual dress culture** consistent with our remote-first, collaborative environment. We trust employees to present themselves professionally in contexts that reflect on TechNova, while maintaining personal comfort and self-expression during day-to-day work.

---

## General Philosophy

TechNova does not require formal business attire. Our dress expectations are guided by context:

- **Daily remote work**: No dress requirements; employees are free to work comfortably from home.
- **Video calls (internal)**: No formal requirements; reasonable personal presentation is appreciated.
- **Video calls (external — customers, partners, investors)**: Business casual is recommended. Clean, neat appearance; TechNova branded swag is always a positive option.
- **In-office visits**: Business casual. Avoid clothing with offensive language, symbols, or imagery.
- **Company events and offsites**: Casual unless otherwise specified in the event invite.
- **Customer on-site visits or conferences**: Business casual to business professional, as appropriate for the customer's culture.

---

## Business Casual Examples

| Context | Appropriate Examples |
|---|---|
| Internal meetings | T-shirts, hoodies, jeans, casual tops |
| Customer-facing video calls | Collared shirts, blouses, smart casual tops |
| In-office | Jeans, chinos, blouses, casual dresses |
| Conferences / events | Smart casual, blazers, business professional |

---

## What to Avoid

Regardless of context, the following are not appropriate in any TechNova work setting (in-office or on video):

- Clothing with explicit, offensive, discriminatory, or sexually suggestive language or imagery
- Clothing that promotes competing commercial products
- Clothing that could be interpreted as political campaigning (unless attending a company-designated civic event)

These standards apply equally to all employees and are consistent with our [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md).

---

## Religious and Cultural Dress

TechNova fully supports and respects employees' rights to wear religious, cultural, or traditional dress in all work settings. Accommodations related to dress for religious or cultural reasons are automatic — no approval is required. If an employee experiences any pushback or discomfort from a colleague related to their attire, they should report it through the [Grievance Procedure](grievance-procedure.md).

---

## TechNova Branded Swag

All employees receive a **TechNova welcome kit** during onboarding that includes branded t-shirts, a hoodie, and a laptop sleeve. Additional swag is distributed at the annual company retreat and available for purchase in the internal Swag Store (swag.technovasolutions.com). Wearing TechNova swag on customer calls and at conferences is encouraged and appreciated.

The company distributes an annual swag budget of **$150 per employee** for ordering from the Swag Store, issued every January.

---

## Questions and Accommodations

Employees with questions about this policy or who need a specific accommodation (medical, religious, or otherwise) should contact HR at hr@technovasolutions.com or reach out to VP HR Lisa Thompson directly. All accommodation requests are handled confidentially.
