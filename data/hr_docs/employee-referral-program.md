# Employee Referral Program

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions' Employee Referral Program (ERP) rewards employees who help recruit exceptional talent. As a ~500-person company growing toward the next stage of our journey, the quality of the people we hire directly shapes the product we build and the culture we maintain. Referrals consistently produce some of our highest-performing hires.

---

## Referral Bonuses

| Role Category | Referral Bonus (Standard) | Referral Bonus (Priority Role*) |
|---|---|---|
| IC1 – IC2 | $2,000 | $3,000 |
| IC3 – IC4 | $3,500 | $5,000 |
| IC5 – IC6 | $5,000 | $7,500 |
| Manager M1–M3 | $5,000 | $7,500 |
| Director / VP | $7,500 | $10,000 |
| Non-Engineering (any level) | $2,000 | $3,000 |

*Priority Roles are flagged in Greenhouse and announced in #talent-updates on Slack. Priority designations are typically roles open for 60+ days or with critical business impact.

---

## Eligibility

- All **full-time TechNova employees** in good standing are eligible to submit referrals
- The referring employee must **still be employed at TechNova** when the referred hire reaches 90 days of employment (the payout threshold)
- The following employees are **not eligible** to receive referral bonuses:
  - Hiring managers for the specific open role
  - HR Recruiting team members involved in the hiring process
  - Members of the Executive team (VP and above)

---

## How to Submit a Referral

1. Obtain the candidate's permission before submitting their information.
2. Log in to **Greenhouse** (greenhouse.io/technovasolutions) and select "Submit a Referral."
3. Fill in the candidate's name, email, role of interest, and a brief note on why you recommend them.
4. The Recruiting team will contact the candidate within **5 business days**.
5. You will receive status updates in Greenhouse and via email at key milestones.

---

## Payout Schedule

| Milestone | Payout Amount |
|---|---|
| Referred hire's first day | 50% of bonus |
| Referred hire completes 90 days | Remaining 50% |

Bonuses are paid through the **next regular payroll cycle** after each milestone is reached. Bonuses are subject to standard income tax withholding.

## Diversity Referral Initiative

In support of our [Diversity & Inclusion](diversity-inclusion.md) goals, TechNova offers an additional **$1,000 diversity referral bonus** for any referred candidate from an underrepresented group (women, non-binary individuals, Black, Latinx, Indigenous, or LGBTQ+ candidates) who is subsequently hired. The additional bonus is paid at the 90-day milestone alongside the standard payout.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I refer someone I know personally, such as a friend or former colleague?**
A: Yes. Most successful referrals come from professional networks and former colleagues. Be transparent with the referral that you are submitting their information.

**Q: What if my referral is rejected for one role but hired for another?**
A: The referral bonus applies to any role the candidate is hired into, as long as your Greenhouse submission is on file before the candidate's first recruiter touchpoint.

**Q: Can I refer a former TechNova employee?**
A: Yes, if at least 12 months have passed since their departure and they left in good standing. Former employees returning without a referral are also eligible (boomerang hires) but do not trigger a referral bonus.

**Q: Is there a limit to how many referrals I can submit?**
A: No. There is no cap on the number of referrals or bonuses an employee can earn in a calendar year.
