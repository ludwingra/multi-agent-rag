# Grievance Procedure

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Purpose

This Grievance Procedure provides TechNova Solutions employees with a clear, fair, and confidential process to raise workplace concerns, complaints, or disputes. TechNova is committed to resolving grievances promptly and equitably, in a manner consistent with our core values of Transparency and Collaboration.

---

## Scope

This procedure applies to all full-time and part-time employees of TechNova Solutions across all office locations (San Francisco, Austin, London, Toronto) and remote employees. It covers, but is not limited to:

- Interpersonal conflicts with colleagues or managers
- Alleged violations of the [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md)
- Workplace harassment or discrimination (see [Diversity & Inclusion](diversity-inclusion.md))
- Disputes over compensation, benefits, or working conditions
- Concerns about management decisions perceived as unfair
- Retaliation concerns

Grievances related to performance evaluations should first follow the process outlined in the [Performance Review Process](performance-review-process.md).

---

## Informal Resolution (Step 1)

Before escalating formally, employees are encouraged to attempt an **informal resolution**:

1. If comfortable doing so, speak directly with the person involved to resolve the issue.
2. Alternatively, discuss the situation with your direct manager.
3. If the concern involves your manager, speak with your skip-level manager or contact the HR Business Partner for your team.

Informal resolution is not mandatory. Employees may proceed directly to Step 2 if the situation warrants it.

**Timeframe**: Informal resolution should be attempted within **10 business days** of the triggering event.

---

## Formal Grievance (Step 2)

If informal resolution does not succeed or is not appropriate, the employee submits a **formal written grievance**:

1. Submit the grievance in writing via email to **hr@technovasolutions.com** or via the online form at hr.technovasolutions.com/grievance.
2. The written grievance should include:
   - Description of the issue and relevant events (dates, names, locations)
   - The resolution or outcome being sought
   - Any supporting evidence (screenshots, messages, documents)
3. HR acknowledges receipt within **2 business days**.
4. An HR Business Partner is assigned to investigate.

---

## Investigation Process (Step 3)

The HR Business Partner will conduct a fair and impartial investigation:

- **Timeline**: Investigations are completed within **20 business days** of grievance submission, unless complexity requires an extension (communicated to all parties).
- Interviews are conducted with the complainant, the respondent, and relevant witnesses.
- All parties are asked to maintain confidentiality during the investigation.
- The complainant and respondent may each bring a support person (colleague or HR advisor) to interviews.

---

## Resolution and Outcome (Step 4)

Upon completion of the investigation, HR will:

1. Prepare a written summary of findings.
2. Recommend an outcome to the relevant VP (or CEO for VP-level disputes).
3. Communicate the decision to the complainant and respondent in writing within **5 business days** of investigation completion.

Possible outcomes include:
- No finding of violation
- Informal corrective guidance to the respondent
- Formal written warning
- Mandatory training or coaching
- Role change, demotion, or termination (see [Termination Process](termination-process.md))

---

## Appeal Process (Step 5)

If either party disagrees with the outcome, they may file an appeal within **10 business days** of receiving the decision. Appeals are reviewed by:

- VP HR Lisa Thompson (if not already the decision-maker), OR
- CEO Sarah Chen for appeals involving VP-level employees

The appeal decision is final and binding within TechNova's internal process.

---

## Anti-Retaliation

TechNova strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who submits a grievance in good faith, participates as a witness, or appeals a decision. Retaliation is a serious [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md) violation and may result in termination.

---

## Record Keeping

Grievance records, including all submissions, investigation notes, and outcomes, are maintained securely by HR for **7 years** and are accessible only to HR personnel and Legal.
