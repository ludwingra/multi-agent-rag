# Internal Transfers Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions encourages internal mobility as a key pillar of employee development and retention. This policy outlines the process for employees who wish to transfer to a different team, department, or office location within TechNova.

Internal mobility allows TechNova to leverage existing talent, reduce hiring costs, and provide employees with growth paths that align with their interests and skills.

---

## Eligibility

To be eligible for an internal transfer, an employee must:

- Have completed at least **12 months in their current role** at TechNova
- Be in good standing (no active Performance Improvement Plan or formal disciplinary process)
- Have a performance rating of **3 (Meets Expectations)** or higher in the most recent review cycle (see [Performance Review Process](performance-review-process.md))

Exceptions to the 12-month tenure requirement may be granted by VP HR Lisa Thompson in cases of organizational restructuring or exceptional business need.

---

## Types of Internal Transfers

| Transfer Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Lateral transfer** | Same level, different team or department |
| **Promotional transfer** | Move to a higher level or more senior role in another team |
| **Geographic transfer** | Same or different role in a different TechNova office (SF, Austin, London, Toronto) |
| **Functional pivot** | Change in role function (e.g., engineering to product, sales to customer success) |

## Process

### Step 1: Explore and Apply
- Browse open roles in **Greenhouse** (internal job board) under the "Internal Opportunities" tab
- Reach out informally to the hiring manager if you have questions about fit before applying
- Submit a formal application in Greenhouse, indicating it is an internal application

### Step 2: Notify Your Current Manager
Before or within 24 hours of submitting a formal application, **notify your current manager** of your interest. TechNova does not support "stealth" applications within the company — transparent communication is expected in line with our value of Transparency.

Your current manager may not block your application. If you experience pressure to withdraw, contact HR immediately.

### Step 3: Interview Process
Internal candidates follow a streamlined interview process:
- Typically 2–3 rounds (vs. 4–5 for external candidates)
- The hiring manager and team assess role fit and technical competencies
- HR reviews the candidate's internal track record (performance ratings, Lattice goals)

### Step 4: Offer and Transition
If selected:
- The hiring manager and HR negotiate the transfer effective date — typically **4–6 weeks** after offer acceptance to allow for knowledge transfer
- Compensation is reviewed: if the new role warrants a different band per [Compensation Bands](compensation-bands.md), an adjustment takes effect on the transfer date
- A formal transition plan is created with the outgoing and incoming managers

---

## Geographic Transfers

Employees requesting a transfer to a different TechNova office must:
- Have a role available at that location, OR work with HR to confirm remote eligibility for the destination office's team
- Acknowledge that compensation may be adjusted based on geographic pay factors (see [Compensation Bands](compensation-bands.md))
- Coordinate relocation independently; TechNova provides a **$5,000 one-time relocation assistance payment** for office-to-office geographic transfers (taxable income)

---

## Functional Pivots

Employees seeking to change functions (e.g., from engineering to product management) should:
1. Discuss career goals with their manager and HR Business Partner
2. Complete relevant training via the [Training & Development](training-development.md) program
3. Apply for open roles in the target function when eligible

Functional pivots may involve a level reassessment to align with competencies in the new function.
