# Mental Health Resources

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions recognizes that mental health is as important as physical health. The demands of working in a fast-paced SaaS environment can be challenging. We are committed to providing meaningful support resources and fostering a culture where asking for help is seen as a sign of strength, not weakness.

---

## Mental Health Days

All TechNova employees receive **3 paid mental health days per year**, separate from sick leave and vacation PTO. These days can be taken proactively — you do not need to be in crisis to use them.

- No justification is required; simply mark the day as "Mental Health Day" in **BambooHR**
- Notify your manager via Slack in advance when possible
- Mental health days do not carry over at year end
- Refer to the [Sick Leave Policy](sick-leave-policy.md) for general sick leave entitlements

---

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP) — Spring Health

TechNova partners with **Spring Health**, a premier mental health EAP, to provide every employee and their household members with:

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Therapy sessions | 8 free sessions per year with a licensed therapist |
| Psychiatry | 3 free appointments per year with a board-certified psychiatrist |
| Coaching | Unlimited coaching sessions (career, relationships, stress) |
| Crisis support | 24/7 crisis line: 1-800-764-SPRING |
| Digital tools | Mindfulness exercises, CBT tools, mood tracking in the Spring Health app |

Access Spring Health at: app.springhealth.com (use your TechNova email to register). All sessions are strictly confidential and not visible to TechNova HR or management.

## Aetna Mental Health Coverage

Beyond the EAP's 8 free sessions, TechNova's Aetna health plans include in-network mental health coverage:

- **Aetna Select PPO**: Standard co-pay of $30/session for in-network therapists
- **Aetna HDHP**: HSA-eligible; sessions apply toward deductible
- Use Aetna's provider finder at aetna.com to locate in-network mental health providers

For details on health plan options, see the [Benefits Overview](benefits-overview.md).

## Manager Resources

Managers play a critical role in supporting team mental health. TechNova provides:

- **Manager training**: Annual "Supporting Your Team's Mental Health" workshop (2 hours, facilitated by Spring Health clinical staff), tracked in Confluence
- **Manager Slack channel**: #manager-support — a confidential space for managers to discuss team wellbeing concerns with HR
- **Mental Health First Aid certification**: Sponsored by TechNova for up to 20 managers annually. Express interest in #people-ops.

---

## Company Practices That Support Mental Health

TechNova builds mental health into how we operate:

- **No-meeting Fridays**: Company policy — internal recurring meetings are not scheduled on Fridays
- **Core hours design**: Meetings are concentrated in the 10 AM – 3 PM window to protect focus time
- **Async-first culture**: Slack messages do not require immediate responses outside of core hours
- **Annual retreat**: Includes wellness programming (yoga, meditation, nature walks) alongside business content
- **Parental leave**: 16 weeks fully paid — see [Parental Leave Policy](parental-leave-policy.md)
- **Wellness reimbursement**: Up to $600/year for gym memberships, meditation apps (Calm, Headspace), fitness classes

## Crisis Situations

If you or a colleague are experiencing a mental health emergency:

- **Spring Health 24/7 Crisis Line**: 1-800-764-SPRING
- **National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: Call or text 988 (US)
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **Emergency**: Call 911 or your local emergency number

If you are concerned about a colleague's safety, contact HR immediately at hr@technovasolutions.com or reach out to VP HR Lisa Thompson directly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will my manager know if I use Spring Health or take a mental health day?**
A: Spring Health sessions are completely confidential. Mental health days in BambooHR appear only as "Leave" to your manager — no reason is shown.

**Q: Can I use my wellness reimbursement for a meditation app subscription?**
A: Yes. Apps like Calm, Headspace, and Waking Up are eligible. Submit receipts via Concur.

**Q: What if 8 Spring Health sessions aren't enough?**
A: Additional sessions are covered by your Aetna mental health benefit at the applicable co-pay rate.
