# Onboarding Guide

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Welcome to TechNova Solutions

Congratulations on joining TechNova Solutions! We are thrilled to have you on board. This guide walks you through your first 90 days at TechNova and helps you navigate our tools, culture, and processes so you can hit the ground running.

TechNova builds **FlowEngine**, our enterprise workflow automation platform used by hundreds of B2B customers worldwide. Understanding our product, our people, and our processes is the foundation of a successful start.

---

## Pre-Boarding Checklist (Before Day 1)

Your recruiter will send you a pre-boarding package via **Greenhouse** after your offer is signed. Please complete the following before your start date:

- [ ] Sign offer letter and NDAs via DocuSign
- [ ] Complete I-9 / employment eligibility verification
- [ ] Submit banking details for direct deposit (via BambooHR)
- [ ] Order home office equipment via IT request form (see [Remote Work Policy](remote-work-policy.md))
- [ ] Enroll in benefits via Aetna portal (deadline: within 30 days of start)

## Week 1: Orientation

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Kickoff with your manager; IT setup (MacBook, VPN, Slack) |
| Day 2 | HR orientation with Lisa Thompson's team (benefits, policies) |
| Day 3 | Meet your team; Jira/Confluence access provisioned |
| Day 4 | FlowEngine product demo with Product team |

## 30 / 60 / 90 Day Goals

### 30 Days
- Complete all required compliance training (assigned in Confluence)
- Understand your team's OKRs for the current quarter
- Make at least one small contribution to your team's Jira board

### 60 Days
- Be an active, independent contributor to sprint work
- Complete your first 1:1 performance check-in with your manager in Lattice
- Introduce yourself in a company All-Hands meeting

### 90 Days
- Demonstrate ownership of at least one deliverable
- Complete onboarding feedback survey (sent by HR via BambooHR)
- Review your 90-day plan with manager and align on next quarter's objectives

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When does my benefits coverage start?**
A: Aetna health coverage begins on your first day of employment. You have 30 days to enroll. See the [Benefits Overview](benefits-overview.md) for details.

**Q: When do I receive my first paycheck?**
A: TechNova runs bi-weekly payroll. Your first paycheck will arrive on the next scheduled pay date after your start date.

**Q: Can I use PTO in my first 90 days?**
A: PTO accrues from day one but can only be used after the 90-day probationary period. See the [Vacation Policy](vacation-policy.md).

**Q: Where do I report a technical issue?**
A: Post in #it-help on Slack or submit a ticket via the IT request form in Jira.
