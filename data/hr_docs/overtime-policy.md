# Overtime Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions values sustainable work practices and the wellbeing of its employees. This Overtime Policy defines eligibility for overtime pay, how overtime hours are tracked, and the expectations around extended work hours at TechNova.

---

## Exempt vs. Non-Exempt Classification

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and applicable state laws, TechNova employees are classified as either **exempt** or **non-exempt**:

| Classification | Description | Overtime Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Exempt** | Salaried professionals meeting FLSA salary/duties tests (most engineers, managers, and senior staff) | Not eligible for FLSA overtime pay |
| **Non-Exempt** | Hourly or certain salaried roles below FLSA threshold | Eligible for 1.5x pay for hours worked over 40/week |

The majority of TechNova's workforce is salaried exempt. Classification is confirmed in your offer letter and BambooHR profile. If you are unsure of your classification, contact hr@technovasolutions.com.

---

## Overtime for Non-Exempt Employees

Non-exempt employees who work more than **40 hours in a workweek** (Sunday midnight to Saturday midnight) are entitled to overtime pay at **1.5x their regular hourly rate** for each overtime hour.

- California-based non-exempt employees are also entitled to daily overtime (1.5x for hours over 8/day, 2x for hours over 12/day), per California Labor Code.
- All overtime hours must be **pre-approved in writing** by the employee's manager before being worked. Unauthorized overtime that is nevertheless worked will still be compensated, but may result in disciplinary action.
- Non-exempt employees must log all hours daily in **BambooHR** (time-tracking module).

---

## Expectations for Exempt Employees

While exempt employees are not entitled to overtime pay, TechNova does not expect or encourage regularly excessive work hours. Our standard expectation is approximately **40 hours per week**.

If project demands temporarily require extended hours:
- Managers should communicate transparently about the expected duration
- Employees who work significantly beyond 40 hours for sustained periods (3+ weeks) should flag this to their manager and HR Business Partner
- Managers may offer **compensatory time off ("comp time")** at their discretion to acknowledge sustained extra effort (this is not a legal entitlement for exempt employees but is encouraged as a cultural practice at TechNova)

## On-Call and Incident Response

Engineers participating in the on-call rotation for FlowEngine infrastructure are compensated through the following structure:

| On-Call Type | Compensation |
|---|---|
| On-call standby (no pages) | $150/week |
| After-hours page (responded to and resolved < 1 hr) | $75/incident |
| After-hours page (resolved 1–3 hrs) | $150/incident |
| After-hours page (resolved > 3 hrs) | $200/incident + following morning off |

On-call rotations are managed in PagerDuty and coordinated by VP Engineering Raj Patel's team. On-call compensation is processed through payroll and reported in BambooHR.

---

## Preventing Overwork

TechNova's remote-first culture requires active effort to prevent "always-on" expectations. All employees are encouraged to:

- Set clear working hours in their Slack profile and Google Calendar
- Decline non-urgent meetings outside core hours (10 AM – 3 PM local time)
- Disconnect completely during vacation (see [Vacation Policy](vacation-policy.md))
- Use the "Do Not Disturb" feature in Slack outside working hours

Managers who consistently schedule meetings or send messages outside employees' stated working hours may be counseled by HR.

---

## Compliance

TechNova complies with all federal, state, and local wage and hour laws, including:
- California Labor Code (for employees in San Francisco and Austin)
- Ontario Employment Standards Act (for Toronto employees)
- UK Working Time Regulations (for London employees — maximum 48 hours average per week unless opted out)
