# Parental Leave Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions is committed to supporting employees through one of life's most significant milestones: welcoming a new child. This policy provides **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** for all eligible employees, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, or family structure.

---

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed at least **6 months of continuous employment** at TechNova Solutions are eligible for paid parental leave. Leave may be taken for:

- Birth of a child
- Adoption of a child (under 18 years old)
- Foster placement of a child

Part-time employees (20+ hours/week) are eligible for prorated paid leave proportional to their scheduled hours.

---

## Leave Entitlement

| Leave Type | Duration | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Primary caregiver leave | 16 weeks | 100% base salary |
| Secondary caregiver leave | 8 weeks | 100% base salary |
| Additional unpaid leave | Up to 8 weeks | Unpaid (job-protected) |

TechNova does not designate "primary" or "secondary" based on gender. Both parents may simultaneously take leave if desired; each household is entitled to a total combined leave benefit.

---

## Concurrent Benefits

During parental leave:
- Health, dental, and vision benefits through **Aetna** continue uninterrupted
- 401(k) employer match (via **Fidelity**) continues to vest
- Equity vesting (via **Carta**) continues during paid leave; vesting pauses during unpaid extension leave unless agreed otherwise with HR
- PTO does **not** accrue during leave periods longer than 2 weeks

---

## How to Request Parental Leave

1. Notify your manager and HR at least **8 weeks before** the expected leave start date (or as soon as practicable for unplanned circumstances such as early birth or emergency adoption).
2. Submit a parental leave request in **BambooHR** and attach supporting documentation (e.g., expected due date letter, adoption placement notice).
3. HR will confirm eligibility and provide a leave agreement within 5 business days.
4. Work with your manager to create a **coverage and transition plan** at least 4 weeks before departure.

---

## Return to Work

Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed:

- **The same or equivalent role** at the same compensation level
- A phased re-entry option: weeks 1–2 at 50% capacity, weeks 3–4 at 75%, then full capacity (upon mutual agreement)
- A 1:1 re-onboarding meeting with your manager and HR partner
- Access to a **lactation room** in all four TechNova offices (coordinates shared via #people-ops Slack channel)
- Continuation of flexible scheduling for the first 3 months post-leave

---

## Coordination with State and Federal Leave

TechNova leave benefits are **supplemental** to any applicable state or federal leave (FMLA, California CFRA, California SDI/PFL, Ontario ESA, UK Statutory Maternity/Paternity Pay). In cases where state benefits provide partial wage replacement, TechNova tops up to 100% of base salary.

Employees should contact HR to understand the interaction between TechNova leave and applicable state programs.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I split my parental leave into multiple periods?**
A: Yes, with manager and HR approval. Leave may be taken in no more than 2 separate blocks within 12 months of the child's birth or placement.

**Q: What if my child is born prematurely or has medical complications?**
A: TechNova will work with you to accommodate extended leave. Contact HR immediately, and additional unpaid leave or disability accommodation may be available.

**Q: Does parental leave affect my performance review?**
A: No. Employees on parental leave are not penalized in performance reviews. Managers will consider only the time during which the employee was actively working.
