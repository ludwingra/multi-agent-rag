# Performance Review Process

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions conducts **bi-annual performance reviews** in June and December to evaluate employee contributions, align on goals, and inform compensation and promotion decisions. The process is managed through **Lattice**, our performance management platform.

---

## Review Cycles

| Review Cycle | Kickoff Date | Self-Review Deadline | Manager Review Deadline | Calibration | Results Shared |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-Year (H1) | May 15 | May 30 | June 10 | June 15–20 | June 30 |
| Year-End (H2) | November 15 | November 30 | December 10 | December 15–20 | December 31 |

---

## Who Participates

- All full-time employees who have been at TechNova for **at least 90 days** by the review kickoff date
- Managers complete reviews for all direct reports
- Employees complete a **self-review** prior to the manager review
- For IC4 and above, **360-degree feedback** is collected from 3–5 peers selected by the employee and approved by their manager

---

## Review Components

### 1. Self-Review
Employees reflect on their performance against:
- OKR attainment for the period
- Core values demonstration (Innovation, Transparency, Collaboration, Customer Obsession)
- Individual growth and areas for development

### 2. Peer Feedback (IC4+)
Peers provide structured feedback on collaboration, technical skills, and impact. Responses are anonymous unless the reviewer opts to share their name.

### 3. Manager Review
Managers assess:
- Overall performance rating (see scale below)
- Goal attainment (0–100%)
- Strengths and development areas
- Promotion readiness (if applicable)

---

## Performance Ratings

| Rating | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Significantly exceeds expectations; role model for the team |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Consistently delivers above the bar for their level |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Reliably delivers what is expected at their level |
| 2 | Partially Meets | Some gaps; a performance improvement plan may be initiated |
| 1 | Does Not Meet | Significant performance concerns; immediate action required |

---

## Calibration

After manager reviews are submitted, all people managers in a department participate in a **calibration session** facilitated by HR (VP HR Lisa Thompson's team). The goal is to ensure rating consistency and fairness across teams.

- Calibration sessions are held over 2 days per department
- Managers present their distribution and discuss edge cases
- Final ratings are locked after calibration
- Individual ratings are **never shared** between peers

---

## Engineering Levels and Promotion

TechNova uses the following engineering leveling framework:

| Track | Levels |
|---|---|
| Individual Contributor | IC1, IC2, IC3, IC4, IC5, IC6 |
| Management | M1, M2, M3 |
| Director | D1, D2 |
| VP | VP |

Promotions are recommended during the Year-End cycle and take effect on **January 1** of the following year. Compensation adjustments accompanying promotions are communicated alongside the review results. Refer to the [Compensation Bands](compensation-bands.md) document for salary ranges by level.

---

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

Employees receiving a rating of 2 or below may be placed on a **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**:

- Duration: 60–90 days
- Goals, check-in cadence, and success criteria are documented in Lattice
- HR is involved in all PIP processes
- Successful completion of a PIP is noted in the employee record

For process details relating to termination following an unsuccessful PIP, see the [Termination Process](termination-process.md).
