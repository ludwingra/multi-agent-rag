# Remote Work Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions has been a **remote-first company since 2021**. We believe that talented people do their best work when they have the flexibility to design their environment. This policy establishes expectations, tooling standards, and guidelines for remote work across all TechNova offices and distributed team members.

---

## Remote-First Philosophy

All roles at TechNova Solutions are remote-eligible by default unless specifically designated as on-site (e.g., certain IT infrastructure roles in our San Francisco HQ). We maintain physical offices in:

| Location | Type | Address |
|---|---|---|
| San Francisco, CA | HQ | 450 Mission Street, Suite 800 |
| Austin, TX | Engineering Hub | 700 Lavaca Street, Suite 1400 |
| London, UK | Sales Office | 1 Canada Square, Canary Wharf |
| Toronto, CA | Support Center | 100 King Street West, Suite 5600 |

---

## Recommended In-Office Days

While attendance is **never mandatory**, TechNova recommends **Tuesday and Thursday** as in-office days for employees near a TechNova office. These days tend to have the highest attendance and facilitate organic collaboration, whiteboarding sessions, and team lunches.

---

## Equipment and Home Office

TechNova provides each employee a **$1,500 home office setup stipend** upon joining, refreshed every 3 years. This covers monitors, keyboards, webcams, headsets, and ergonomic accessories. Purchases must be submitted through Jira (IT request queue) and reimbursed via Concur within 30 days.

Additionally, employees receive a **$100/month connectivity stipend** to cover internet and phone costs, paid monthly through payroll.

### Standard Issued Equipment

All employees receive:
- Apple MacBook Pro (14" or 16" depending on role)
- USB-C dock
- Logitech webcam (HD 1080p)

---

## Communication and Availability

- **Core hours**: 10 AM – 3 PM in the employee's local time zone, Monday through Friday.
- Employees are expected to be reachable on **Slack** during core hours and respond within 2 hours.
- All meetings must have a video link (Zoom or Google Meet) — no phone-only meetings for internal calls.
- Camera-on is encouraged but never required.
- Use **Slack status** to communicate availability (e.g., "In a meeting," "Focus time," "Offline – back at 3 PM").

---

## Security and Compliance

Employees working remotely must adhere to TechNova's security standards:

1. Use the company-provided **VPN (Tailscale)** when accessing internal systems.
2. Lock your screen when stepping away from your workstation.
3. Never work from public Wi-Fi without VPN active.
4. Report lost or stolen devices to IT Security immediately via #it-security on Slack.
5. Do not store confidential client data on personal devices.

Refer to the [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md) for information on data privacy obligations.

---

## International Remote Work

Employees may work from abroad for up to **30 consecutive calendar days per year** without triggering tax or employment law issues, subject to manager approval. Requests exceeding 30 days require review by the Legal and HR teams. Contact hr@technovasolutions.com to initiate a review.
