# Sick Leave Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions provides paid sick leave to support employees when they are unwell or need to care for an ill family member. This policy covers the entitlement, process for reporting absences, and how sick leave interacts with other TechNova leave programs.

---

## Sick Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees receive **12 paid sick days per calendar year** (January 1 – December 31). Sick days are available in full at the beginning of each calendar year and do not require prior accrual.

| Employee Type | Annual Sick Days |
|---|---|
| Full-time (40 hrs/week) | 12 days |
| Part-time (20–39 hrs/week) | Prorated |
| Contractors | Not eligible |

Unused sick days **do not carry over** to the following year and are **not paid out** upon termination.

---

## Eligible Uses of Sick Leave

Sick leave may be used for:

- Personal illness, injury, or medical appointments
- Mental health days (up to 3 days per year are designated as mental health days — see [Mental Health Resources](mental-health-resources.md))
- Recovery from a medical procedure or surgery
- Caring for an ill immediate family member (spouse/partner, child, parent, sibling)
- Preventive care (doctor, dentist, vision appointments)
- Pregnancy-related conditions (until the start of [Parental Leave](parental-leave-policy.md))

---

## How to Report a Sick Day

1. **Notify your manager** via Slack or email as early as possible, ideally before your standard start time.
2. **Log the absence** in **BambooHR** on the same day or the next business day at the latest.
3. For absences of **3 or more consecutive days**, a doctor's note or equivalent documentation may be requested by HR.
4. For absences of **5 or more consecutive days**, HR will evaluate eligibility for short-term disability coverage through Aetna.

---

## Long-Term Illness and Disability

If an employee is unable to work for an extended period due to illness or injury:

- **Short-term disability (STD)**: Aetna provides income replacement at **60% of base salary** after a 5-day waiting period, for up to 12 weeks.
- **Long-term disability (LTD)**: If the condition extends beyond 12 weeks, Aetna LTD coverage provides 60% of base salary for up to 24 months.
- **FMLA**: TechNova complies with the Family and Medical Leave Act, providing up to 12 weeks of job-protected unpaid leave per year for qualifying conditions.

Employees on disability leave continue to receive health benefits through Aetna. 401(k) contributions pause but employer match vesting continues.

---

## Interaction with Vacation Leave

Sick leave and vacation leave are separate entitlements:
- Employees may **not** use vacation days in place of sick days if sick leave is available.
- If sick leave is exhausted, employees may request to use vacation days to cover additional medical absences, subject to manager approval.
- Sick leave taken during approved vacation will convert those vacation days back to PTO (requires a doctor's note).

---

## Confidentiality

Medical information shared with HR is treated as strictly confidential and stored separately from the general personnel file. Managers are informed of absences but are **not** provided medical diagnoses.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do my 12 sick days reset on January 1 regardless of when I joined?**
A: Yes. New employees hired mid-year receive a prorated number of sick days based on their start date.

**Q: Can I use sick days for a medical appointment that takes only 2 hours?**
A: Yes. Sick leave may be taken in half-day (0.5 day) increments for appointments.

**Q: What if I need more than 12 sick days in a year?**
A: After exhausting sick leave, you may use vacation PTO, apply for Aetna STD, or request unpaid leave. Contact HR to discuss options.
