## Overview

TechNova Solutions offers equity compensation as a core component of our total compensation package. This guide explains how stock options and RSUs work at TechNova, managed through **Carta**. We encourage all employees to read this document carefully and consult a personal financial advisor for tax guidance.

---

## Background

TechNova Solutions was founded in 2019 and raised:
- **Series A**: 2020 — $18M
- **Series B**: 2022 — $65M
- The company reached profitability in **Q3 2024** with approximately **$85M ARR**

Equity in a private company like TechNova has real but illiquid value — you cannot sell it until a liquidity event (IPO, acquisition, or secondary sale opportunity). TechNova's Board regularly reviews secondary sale opportunities for employees with meaningful vested equity.

## Types of Equity at TechNova

| Type | Who Receives It | Tax Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)** | IC1–IC4 and most full-time employees | Potentially favorable (AMT implications; consult your advisor) |
| **Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)** | IC5+, M2+, Directors, VPs | Taxed as ordinary income at vesting |
| **Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs)** | Contractors and some advisors | Taxed as ordinary income at exercise |

---

## Vesting Schedule

Standard equity grants vest over **4 years with a 1-year cliff**:

- **Year 1 (cliff)**: 25% vests on the 1-year anniversary of your grant date
- **Years 2–4**: Remaining 75% vests monthly (1/48 per month) over the following 36 months

Example: An employee receiving a $120,000 ISO grant (4-year vest, 1-year cliff) vests $30,000 at their 1-year anniversary, then $2,500/month for 36 months.

4. Contact hr@technovasolutions.com if you need assistance with Carta access

## Exercising Stock Options

When you exercise ISOs, you purchase shares at the **strike price** (the fair market value on your grant date, set by the Board's 409A valuation). Key points:

- Current 409A valuation (as of January 2025): **$8.40/share** (updated annually)
- Exercise requires payment of the strike price per share
- Carta supports cash exercise; Tender Offer / cashless exercise may be available during secondary events
- You have **90 days after leaving TechNova** to exercise vested ISOs before they expire (converting to NSOs at day 91). Post-termination exercise windows for ISOs are strictly governed by IRS rules.

## Liquidity Events

TechNova's equity has value that may be realized through:
- **IPO**: TechNova's Board periodically evaluates market conditions for a public offering
- **Acquisition**: If TechNova is acquired, vested equity typically converts to acquirer equity or cash
- **Secondary tender offers**: TechNova has facilitated one secondary sale (2023) and aims to do so periodically for eligible employees

Employees will receive advance notice of any liquidity event via an All-Hands meeting hosted by CEO Sarah Chen and CFO David Park.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens to my unvested equity if I leave TechNova?**
A: Unvested equity is forfeited upon separation. Only vested equity is exercisable, and you have 90 days to exercise vested ISOs after leaving.

**Q: Does equity continue to vest during parental leave?**
A: Yes, equity vesting continues during paid parental leave. See the [Parental Leave Policy](parental-leave-policy.md) for details.

**Q: What is the current company valuation?**
A: Per the most recent 409A (January 2025), fair market value is $8.40/share. The preferred share price from the Series B is $12.50/share. TechNova's current internal valuation is available under NDA for employees who request it via CFO David Park's office.
