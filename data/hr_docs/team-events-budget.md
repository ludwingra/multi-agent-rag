# Team Events Budget Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions allocates budget for team events to strengthen relationships, foster collaboration, and celebrate achievements across our distributed workforce. As a remote-first company since 2021, intentional in-person and virtual connection is essential to our culture of Collaboration and Innovation.

---

## Budget Structure

Team events budgets are allocated at two levels:

### 1. Team-Level Budget

Each team manager (M1 and above) receives an annual discretionary team events budget:

| Team Size | Annual Budget |
|---|---|
| 3–7 members | $3,000 |
| 8–14 members | $5,500 |
| 15–25 members | $9,000 |
| 26+ members | $12,000 |

This budget covers team dinners, virtual escape rooms, cooking classes, sporting events, holiday parties, and other team-building activities. Budget resets on January 1 and unused funds do not roll over.

### 2. Company-Wide Events

HR organizes and funds the following company-wide events centrally:

| Event | Frequency | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Company Retreat | Once per year (October) | In-person, all ~500 employees |
| All-Hands Meeting | Monthly | Virtual (Zoom) |
| FlowEngine Launch Celebrations | Per major release | Virtual + optional local gatherings |
| End-of-Year Celebration | December | Virtual + optional local gatherings |
| ERG-sponsored events | Per ERG calendar | Virtual or local |

---

## Annual Company Retreat

TechNova's annual retreat is the flagship culture event of the year. Key details:

- **Location**: Rotates annually (2024: Scottsdale AZ; 2025: Nashville TN; 2026: Lisbon, Portugal)
- **Duration**: 3 days / 2 nights
- **Coverage**: 100% company-paid (travel, accommodation, meals, activities)
- **Format**: Keynotes from Sarah Chen (CEO) and leadership team, team workshops, social dinners, optional outdoor activities
- Attendance is **strongly encouraged** but not mandatory. Remote employees who cannot travel should notify their manager via BambooHR at least 6 weeks in advance.

---

## How to Use Team Budget

1. Discuss the event concept with your team (consider inclusivity — see [Diversity & Inclusion](diversity-inclusion.md) for guidance)
2. Submit an event request in **Jira** (Team Events queue) with:
   - Activity description
   - Estimated cost and attendee count
   - Proposed date
3. HR approves requests under $2,000 within 2 business days; requests over $2,000 require VP-level approval
4. Pay via corporate credit card or request a prepaid virtual card from Finance
5. Submit receipts in **Concur** within 15 business days of the event
6. Provide a brief Slack update in #team-events after the activity (optional but encouraged!)

## Eligible and Ineligible Expenses

### Eligible
- Restaurant meals and food delivery for the team
- Virtual event platform fees (e.g., virtual escape rooms, trivia platforms)
- Sports or concert tickets (limit: 1 event per team per quarter)
- Cooking, art, or mixology classes
- Team swag for the event
- Non-alcoholic beverages and alcohol in moderation (no more than $30/person on alcohol)

### Ineligible
- Personal expenses, gifts for individuals
- Events that exclude team members (scheduling must accommodate all time zones)
- Political donations or fundraising events
- Expenses that violate the [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md)

---

## Inclusivity Considerations

Event planners must ensure activities are:
- **Accessible** to team members with physical limitations
- **Inclusive** of dietary restrictions (provide vegetarian, vegan, and allergen-friendly options)
- **Time zone-aware** for distributed teams (virtual events should accommodate all team members' time zones)
- **Non-alcoholic options always available** at any event where alcohol is served
