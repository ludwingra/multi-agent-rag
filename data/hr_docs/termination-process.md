# Termination Process

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson | Confidential

---

## Overview

This document outlines TechNova Solutions' process for separating an employee, whether through voluntary resignation, involuntary termination, or a mutual agreement. TechNova handles all separations with respect, transparency, and in full compliance with applicable employment law.

---

## Types of Separation

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Voluntary Resignation** | Employee elects to leave TechNova |
| **Involuntary Termination (With Cause)** | Employee is terminated for a performance or conduct violation |
| **Involuntary Termination (Without Cause)** | Employee is let go due to role elimination, restructuring, or business needs |
| **Mutual Separation** | Both parties agree to part ways, typically with a severance agreement |
| **End of Contract** | Contractor engagement concludes as scheduled |

---

## Voluntary Resignation

When an employee decides to resign:

1. Submit a written resignation to your direct manager and cc hr@technovasolutions.com.
2. Standard notice period is **2 weeks**. Senior employees (IC5+ or M2+) are encouraged to provide **4 weeks** to support knowledge transfer.
3. HR schedules an **exit interview** within the final week. All feedback is confidential and used to improve TechNova's employee experience.
4. Equipment return: ship or drop off company hardware (MacBook, accessories) within **5 business days** of last day using the prepaid return label IT provides.

---

## Involuntary Termination — With Cause

Terminations for cause follow a progressive discipline process in most cases:

### Progressive Discipline Steps
1. **Verbal warning** — manager, documented in Lattice
2. **Written warning** — manager + HR, signed by employee
3. **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** — 60–90 day structured plan (see [Performance Review Process](performance-review-process.md))
4. **Termination** — if PIP goals are not met or conduct violation is severe

Some violations may bypass progressive discipline and result in **immediate termination**, including:
- Theft or fraud
- Physical violence or threats
- Severe harassment or discrimination (see [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md))
- Unauthorized access or exfiltration of confidential data
- Falsification of records

All terminations require approval from the employee's VP and VP HR Lisa Thompson before execution.

---

## Involuntary Termination — Without Cause

For restructuring-related terminations (role eliminations, RIFs):

- Employees receive a minimum of **2 weeks written notice** or pay in lieu of notice
- Severance is calculated as **2 weeks of base salary per year of service**, minimum 4 weeks, maximum 26 weeks
- COBRA continuation of Aetna health coverage is available for up to 18 months (employee pays full premium)
- Outplacement support (resume review, LinkedIn coaching) provided via the HR partner for 90 days post-separation
- Employees retain any vested equity as of the separation date per the [Stock Options Guide](stock-options-guide.md)

---

## Final Pay and Offboarding

On the last day of employment:

| Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Final paycheck | Issued on the next regular payroll date or earlier per state law |
| Accrued vacation payout | Paid in final paycheck (as per [Vacation Policy](vacation-policy.md)) |
| System access | Revoked at 5 PM local time on last day (Slack, GitHub, AWS, Jira) |
| Equipment return | Prepaid shipping label issued by IT |
| Benefits | Aetna health coverage ends on last day of the month of separation |
| 401(k) | Employee may keep or roll over; Fidelity notified within 5 business days |
| Equity | See [Stock Options Guide](stock-options-guide.md) for post-termination exercise windows |

---

## Confidentiality of Separation

Details of any employee separation are strictly confidential and shared only on a need-to-know basis. Managers must not discuss the circumstances of a termination with team members beyond acknowledging the departure. Refer inquiries to hr@technovasolutions.com.
