# Training & Development

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions invests in the growth of every employee. We believe that continuous learning is essential to maintaining a high-performing engineering organization and a competitive, innovative culture. This document outlines the learning budget, programs, and processes available to all TechNova employees.

---

## Annual Learning Budget

Every full-time employee receives a **$2,000 annual learning and development (L&D) budget**, refreshed on January 1 each year. Unused budget does not roll over.

Eligible expenses include:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Online courses | Coursera, Udemy, Pluralsight, A Cloud Guru, O'Reilly |
| Books and publications | Technical books, industry journals |
| Conferences and workshops | KubeCon, AWS re:Invent, PyCon, React Summit |
| Certifications | AWS, Google Cloud, Kubernetes (CKA/CKAD), Scrum, PMP |
| Coaching and mentoring | Professional coaching sessions |

Non-eligible expenses: personal hobbies, food and travel (separate from conference attendance), equipment (covered by home office stipend — see [Remote Work Policy](remote-work-policy.md)).

---

## How to Use Your L&D Budget

1. Identify a course, conference, or certification aligned to your role and development goals.
2. Discuss with your manager in a 1:1 or via Lattice check-in to confirm alignment.
3. Submit a request in **Jira** (L&D request queue) with cost, provider, and business justification.
4. HR approves requests within 3 business days.
5. Pay out-of-pocket and submit receipts in **Concur** within 30 days of the expense. Reimbursement processes within the next payroll cycle.

For conference travel over $1,000, pre-approval from your manager and Director is required.

## TechNova Internal Learning Programs

Beyond the individual budget, TechNova runs company-wide programs:

### TechNova Academy (Quarterly Workshops)
HR and Engineering co-organize quarterly workshops on topics such as:
- Engineering leadership fundamentals (for M1 candidates)
- Effective code reviews and PR culture
- Python and TypeScript best practices
- Kubernetes and AWS architecture patterns

Sessions are announced in the #learning Slack channel and recorded in Confluence.

### Engineering Mentorship Program
Employees at IC3 and below can apply for a **6-month mentorship pairing** with a senior IC (IC5/IC6) or Manager. Applications open every January and July. Contact HR or post in #mentorship-program on Slack to apply.

### Book Club
A monthly cross-team book club meets on the last Thursday of each month via Zoom. Titles are voted on in Slack. Previous reads include *The Pragmatic Programmer*, *An Elegant Puzzle*, and *Continuous Delivery*.

### Conference Sponsorship
TechNova sponsors up to **4 employees per year** to speak or present at industry conferences, covering registration, travel, and accommodation above the standard L&D budget. Submit a speaker proposal to your VP for nomination.

## Development Planning with Lattice

Each employee is expected to maintain a **Development Plan** in **Lattice** updated at least twice per year (aligned with the bi-annual performance review cycle):

- Define 2–3 growth goals for the next 6 months
- Identify skills to develop and resources (courses, mentors, stretch projects)
- Track progress in monthly 1:1s with your manager

See the [Performance Review Process](performance-review-process.md) for how development goals factor into your review.

## Promotion and Leveling

Career progression at TechNova is transparent and tied to demonstrated competencies for each level (IC1–IC6, M1–M3, D1–D2, VP). Level guides are published in Confluence under "Engineering Leveling Framework." Compensation ranges by level are documented in the [Compensation Bands](compensation-bands.md) guide.
