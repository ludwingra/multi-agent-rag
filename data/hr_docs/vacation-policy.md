# Vacation Policy

**TechNova Solutions** | HR Department | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: VP HR Lisa Thompson

---

## Overview

TechNova Solutions is committed to supporting the wellbeing and work-life balance of all employees. This Vacation Policy outlines the paid time off (PTO) entitlements, accrual rules, and procedures for requesting vacation leave.

---

## PTO Entitlement

All full-time employees receive **25 vacation days per calendar year** (January 1 – December 31), in addition to TechNova's observed company holidays. Part-time employees receive a prorated amount based on their scheduled hours.

| Employee Type | Annual PTO |
|---|---|
| Full-time (40 hrs/week) | 25 days |
| Part-time (20–39 hrs/week) | Prorated |
| Contractors | Not eligible |

---

## Accrual Schedule

PTO accrues at a rate of **2.08 days per month**, beginning on the employee's first day of employment. New hires may use accrued PTO after completing 90 days of service (probationary period).

---

## Carryover and Payout

- Employees may carry over a maximum of **10 unused vacation days** into the following calendar year.
- Days exceeding the 10-day cap are forfeited on December 31.
- Upon voluntary resignation or involuntary termination, accrued and unused vacation days will be paid out at the employee's current daily rate, in accordance with applicable state law.

---

## How to Request Vacation

1. Submit a vacation request in **BambooHR** at least **5 business days** in advance for absences of 1–3 days.
2. Submit at least **15 business days** in advance for absences of 4 or more consecutive days.
3. Your manager will approve or deny the request within 3 business days.
4. Confirm approval before booking non-refundable travel.

Blackout periods may apply around product launches, major releases, or company-wide events. Your manager will communicate these in advance via Slack.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use vacation days during my probationary period?**
A: No. PTO usage is available after completing 90 days of employment.

**Q: What happens if my vacation request overlaps with a colleague's?**
A: Managers prioritize requests on a first-come, first-served basis while ensuring team coverage.

**Q: Can I take vacation in half-day increments?**
A: Yes. Half-day requests (0.5 days) are supported in BambooHR.

**Q: What if I get sick during my approved vacation?**
A: If you require medical attention, you may convert those days to sick leave. See the [Sick Leave Policy](sick-leave-policy.md) for details.
